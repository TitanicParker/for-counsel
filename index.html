Why this site exists

This site exists because the patient was forced to become procedurally competent when the system’s own procedures did not protect him.

Orientation for counsel

The work is a consequence of the case.

The patient had to learn the languages of medicine, data protection, complaint handling, chronology, evidence, redaction, legal theory, and web publication. That is not normal patient labour. It is compensatory labour: the labour created when correction fails internally.

Core proposition. The patient’s present vigilance is a consequence of the case, not a distraction from it.

This matters for how the site should be read. It is not an attempt to overwhelm counsel with volume. It is an attempt to make an already documented case legible. The work exists because the sequence was not made clear by the institutions responsible for the record. The patient has had to build the route through the evidence that the system never provided.

The site is therefore not separate from the history of the case. It is a disciplined response to it.

Having once been governed by a record whose ordering he could not correct, the patient has now built a record whose ordering cannot be missed.

The documentary case

The case is built from the record.

The site does not ask counsel to accept outrage, speculation, or a retrospective genetic argument. It asks counsel to read the documents in sequence. The record accepts the feet at entry, subordinates them at discharge, repeatedly encounters them later, names the misalignment, and then absorbs contradiction without reopening.
