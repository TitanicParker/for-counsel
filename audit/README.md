# Provenance / legal audit

This directory is reserved for the final source-provenance, documentary and legal quality-control material.

The supplied controlling file for the present revision is:

- `source-provenance-documentary-legal-audit.html`

It is **analysis/audit material, not primary evidence**. It controls the earlier analytical reports where it qualifies, corrects, downgrades, removes or reformulates a proposition.

The counsel-facing overview applies its main corrections, including:

- “the accessible record does not demonstrate the reasoning bridge,” rather than asserting no reasoning existed;
- a 2025 note **records** that podiatry made things worse, without converting that into an independent causal finding;
- the later DaTSCAN cannot be back-projected into the 9–11 July 2017 decision state;
- SIMT's decision is established, but **independent revalidation of the disputed foot/podiatry hinge has not yet been demonstrated**;
- Article 16 is framed as accuracy/completion/qualification rather than rewriting historical opinion;
- Article 18 notice is established, while implementation, refusal, qualification, lifting and Article 18(2) handling remain unknown;
- documentary incompleteness may be relevant to statutory date-of-knowledge facts but does not automatically postpone limitation;
- 19 March 2025 is preserved as serious adverse knowledge evidence for the broad historical clinical claim;
- fraud, concealment and section 71 postponement are not pleaded as established facts.

Primary evidence remains separate under [`docs/source-records/`](../docs/source-records/README.md).
