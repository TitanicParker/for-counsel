# Deep-Dive Repository Audit — 12 August 2026

**Scope:** whole-repository reader/evidential alignment review of `agent/counsel-site-rotation`, compared against `main` and `agent/source-extraction-genealogy`.

**Purpose:** test whether a cold reader can enter the repository, understand the case, inspect the proof beneath it, and hear Liam's own evidence without being redirected into stale framing, mismatched propositions, or unsupported narrative compression.

---

# Executive finding

The repository has now developed a strong evidential brain, but the public presentation and control documents are not yet fully aligned with it.

The strongest current architecture is:

**story + proof + patient voice + authority/information flow.**

The back end now contains the essential ingredients:

- double-lock mechanism;
- patient-voice reconstruction rule;
- semantic transformation analysis of `separate feet` language;
- direct first-person evidence concerning the Tuesday morning Complex Case Meeting sequence;
- protest/witness investigation;
- authority-recursion map;
- source genealogy;
- proposition map;
- source acquisition ledger;
- reader architecture.

But those layers have accumulated faster than the public-facing site has been rewritten.

The next phase is therefore not new theory generation. It is **alignment and materialisation**.

# A. What is now genuinely strong

## 1. The case has a coherent mechanism

The strongest narrative no longer depends on a hindsight-misdiagnosis theory.

The repository can now explain the case as:

**open foot/lower-limb presentation → severe Parkinsonism → early treatment lock → unresolved foot relationship → administrative/documentary lock → inherited classification → repeated reopening signals → 2025 therapeutic/knowledge rupture → 2026 PRKN coherence → later institutional reliance.**

That is a substantial improvement over the older `corrigibility/frame-management` abstraction because it gives the reader a causal sequence.

## 2. The source layer is materially developed

The source-record library contains:

- GP referral;
- 2017 discharge/neurology material;
- Monaghan-to-Healy correspondence;
- contemporaneous October 2017 patient email;
- longitudinal Neurology 2018–2025;
- complaint/QPS/Medical Council material;
- SIMT outcomes;
- GDPR/process sources;
- 2026 PRKN specialist conclusion.

The source acquisition ledger clearly separates materialised, inspected, derivative and missing records.

## 3. Source genealogy is now a real strength

The repository correctly distinguishes:

**historical source → later clinician account → PAR/SIMT → later institutional reliance**

and does not count repetition as independent corroboration.

This is especially important for later institutional authority.

## 4. The patient voice has finally entered the evidential architecture

The repository now recognises that once the hinge record is impeached as a complete account, Liam's first-person evidence must be treated as evidence, not as a decorative allegation.

The back end now distinguishes:

- first-person event evidence;
- first-person communication evidence;
- first-person temporal evidence;
- patient interpretation;
- later patient reconstruction.

That is exactly the right evidential move.

## 5. The ambiguity around `separate feet` has been materially clarified

The new patient-language work identifies a possible semantic escalation:

**different experiences → different problems → possibly different causes → different pathways → inherited causal separation.**

The source record itself only reaches `Not clear`.

This is one of the strongest new explanatory findings in the repository.

## 6. Authority is now mapped functionally rather than simplistically

The repo now separates:

- Monaghan: evolving bedside information;
- Counihan: senior local treatment authority/influence;
- Healy: external specialist/neurogenetic authority.

The key question is no longer merely `who was the boss?` but whether changed patient information ever travelled back through the authority structure after the first treatment answer became controlling.

This is a much more precise formulation of the first lock.

## CRITICAL 5 — The strongest Tuesday-morning patient evidence exists only in an analytical audit file, not as a preserved source artefact

`patient-voice-evidential-status.md` records direct first-person evidence that Liam says:

- at approximately 08:00 on Tuesday 4 July 2017 he was taken from his bed to the Complex Case Meeting;
- he was physically present;
- he was examined there including for tremor;
- by approximately 10:00 he was told levodopa would not be used;
- he recalls an explanation involving inability to prove idiopathic Parkinson's during life and that `the boss` had said levodopa could not be used.

This is potentially among the most important first-person evidence in the entire case.

But it is currently embodied in an analytical/governing file rather than a dated source statement.

That violates the repository's own architecture:

**SOURCE → FACT → PROPOSITION → PROSE.**

**Required action:** preserve Liam's account as a standalone dated patient-witness source, in his words, then atomise it and rebuild the affected propositions.

Until that is done, the repo should not silently treat the analytical file as if it were the source itself.
