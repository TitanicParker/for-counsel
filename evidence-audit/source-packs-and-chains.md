# Counsel-Ready Source Packs and Institutional Chains

These are **manifests**, not rewritten evidence. The underlying primary source pages are left untouched. The purpose is to identify the smallest source set from which counsel can reproduce the relevant documentary sequence.

## Pack 1 — 2017 documentary hinge

### H01 — `docs/source-records/gp-referral.html`

This belongs because it fixes the problem presented to the hospital before Neurology supplied its own interpretation: bilateral foot problems were part of an abnormal gait/movement/tone presentation. It is an independent pre-admission source and prevents the feet from being dismissed as a later reconstruction.

### H02 — `docs/source-records/discharge-summary.html`

This is the central hinge source. In one contemporaneous clinical artefact it records severe parkinsonism and atypical/dystonic features, preserves the foot-neurology relationship as `Not clear`, records podiatry referral/disposition, records the treatment-deferral rationale and narrow `good understanding` language, and contains the LTI/MRI/chronology material.

### H05 — `docs/source-records/neurology-notes-2017.html`

This belongs primarily to protect chronological discipline and preserve adverse evidence. It fixes the abnormal DaTSCAN on 13 July and the later addendum time, while also showing that the scan indication itself described a very young, bilateral, atypical and almost dystonic presentation; it cannot be back-projected into earlier operative decisions.

### Minimum reproducible chain

**PRESENTING FEET** — H01  
→ **NEUROLOGICAL FINDINGS / UNCERTAINTY** — H02  
→ **`NOT CLEAR`** — H02  
→ **PODIATRY DISPOSITION** — H02  
→ **LATER DATSCAN / ADDENDUM** — H05.

**Smallest possible set for the core transition:** H01 + H02. H05 should accompany it for chronology and the adverse parkinsonism evidence.

**What this pack does not prove:** negligence; that podiatry was inappropriate; that Parkinson's was an unreasonable working diagnosis; that levodopa should have started in 2017; that the feet were neurological; that Liam protested; or that any actor was dishonest.

## Pack 2 — Continuity and materiality

This pack is deliberately small and does not attempt to prove 2017 breach by hindsight.

### C01 — `docs/source-records/neurology-notes-2018.html`

The 2018 note shows that foot symptoms remained active within Neurology and changed with medication withdrawal: the outside-left-foot phenomenon returned rapidly when procyclidine was stopped. It also records continued deliberate deferral of levodopa despite an expectation that it could make Liam substantially better, preserving both the response signal and the adverse treatment-caution evidence.

### C03 — `docs/source-records/neurology-notes-2023.html`

This is the minimum source for later dystonia/OFF and disagreement. It records bilateral foot difficulty, an ON/OFF dystonia question, a likely OFF interpretation, levodopa/Stalevo-related functional effects, the explicit `misalignment`, and the acknowledgement that the earliest symptoms were in the feet.

### C05 — `docs/source-records/neurology-notes-2025.html`

This fixes the foot problem as a major current Neurology issue in 2025, records severe morning symptoms and relief once Parkinson's medication took effect, and documents a Sinemet CR trial directed at the morning problem. It simultaneously preserves the clinician's competing view that the pain was not, in his assessment, directly Parkinsonian and that increasing dopaminergic treatment carried dyskinesia risk.

### Optional corroborative continuity source — C04

`docs/source-records/neurology-notes-2024.html` adds toe-curling persistence and the distinction between pain relief and continued motor phenomena. It is useful but not necessary to the minimum pack.

### Minimum reproducible continuity chain

2018 persistent/medication-sensitive foot phenomena  
→ 2023 recurrent bilateral foot problem + OFF/dystonia question + recorded misalignment  
→ 2025 severe foot problem remains within Neurology + dopaminergic modification / CR trial.

**Purpose:** continuity and materiality only. **Not retrospective proof of 2017 negligence.**

The repository does not yet contain a self-sufficient provenance pack. The following project-archive sources are required.

### P01 — 2017 Monaghan correspondence (`Myself and Tim`)

This is the key contemporaneous provenance source because it indicates a consultative/collective treatment position rather than a purely individual decision. The native image/header/date should be preserved when migrated; the document by itself does not identify `Tim` with complete self-authentication.

### P04 — Counihan response, 7 May 2025

This is needed because it records Counihan's own later description of his role/non-role. It is contrary/qualifying material and must travel with the provenance pack rather than being omitted because later Monaghan material points to advice.

### P05 — Monaghan Medical Council submission, 29 Jul 2025

This is needed because Monaghan later identifies the consultative context and Counihan advice and refers to the Complex Case Meeting. It is a later treating-clinician representation, not independent proof of the 2017 event.

### P06 — Medical Council decision, 29 Jul 2025

This belongs because it records the regulator's narrower formulation that Counihan was not `directly involved` in care. It proves a regulatory decision and wording, not an independent reconstruction of the 2017 authority chain.

### Missing source

**P07 — Complex Case Meeting minute/attendance/source material — MISSING — CONTROLLING EVIDENCE GAP.**

Without P07, counsel can reproduce the existence of a provenance conflict and strong evidence of consultation, but cannot safely determine exact roles, final authority, attendees, scope of advice or actor-specific knowledge.
