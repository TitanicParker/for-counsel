## Material that remains useful despite superseded theory

The older pages should not be erased merely because their framing is superseded. They contain useful source locators, chronology, links and historical development of the project. Their future value is best preserved as **archived analytical material**, with the primary source pages remaining untouched.

Particularly useful material to retain:

- source links embedded in `docs/index.html` and `docs/documented-on-the-face.html`;
- chronology and primary quotations where checked against source pages;
- `gdpr-incorrigibility.html` factual timeline, especially its explicit warning that Article 18 invocation does not itself prove breach;
- `docs/issues-for-counsel.html` disclosure requests and expert questions, after terminology is narrowed;
- historical theory pages as an audit trail of project development, not as counsel's evidential starting point.

# Final reproducibility test

> **If every analytical document were deleted tomorrow, could competent counsel reconstruct the central case from the source repository alone?**

## Current answer: **NO — but the core clinical documentary hinge is substantially reproducible.**

### What counsel can reproduce now from `for-counsel` alone

- bilateral feet were part of the presenting problem;
- substantial parkinsonism was present;
- young/atypical/dystonic-looking features were recorded;
- the record itself preserved the foot relationship as `Not clear`;
- podiatry became an operative disposition;
- levodopa was deliberately deferred with a recorded rationale and narrow `good understanding` statement;
- the later DaTSCAN was abnormal and temporally later;
- foot symptoms persisted through Neurology;
- later dystonia/OFF questions and explicit misalignment appeared;
- later dopaminergic treatment modified the foot problem.

### What counsel cannot yet reproduce from `for-counsel` alone

1. **Exact 2017 decision provenance** — because the native `Myself and Tim`/Healy material and Complex Case Meeting record are not in the repository.
2. **Whether the bedside protest occurred** — because no independent trainee/witness/teaching source has been located.
3. **Exact native discharge/version chronology** — because EHR versions/metadata are missing.
4. **Whether PAR/SIMT independently revalidated the foot/podiatry hinge** — because the complete PAR, source schedule/attachments and SIMT minutes/pack are missing.
5. **What operational effect the Article 18 request had** — because DPO/QPS routing/restriction/audit records are missing.
6. **The complete knowledge/notice and GDPR/governance chain from repository sources alone** — because several primary emails, decisions and regulatory documents remain only in the project archive/Gmail.

Until those sources are migrated or disclosed, the repository can independently reproduce the **clinical documentary problem and longitudinal materiality**, but not the full **provenance, governance, GDPR operational state or protest corroboration** without reliance on the wider project archive.
