## Immediate inventory conclusion

The repository is now comparatively strong on the **clinical chronology** and materially improved on **decision provenance and later governance provenance**, but remains weak on the hospital's internal source architecture. The source pages allow an independent reader to reproduce: foot-centred presentation; severe parkinsonism; atypical/dystonic features; `Not clear`; podiatry disposition; deliberate levodopa deferral; a reproduced contemporaneous `Myself and Tim` / Healy advice request; later Monaghan identification of Counihan advice; later medication-sensitive foot symptoms; 2023 OFF/dystonia/misalignment material; 2025 foot-focused treatment; QPS/SIMT decisions; and 2026 PRKN/Parkin specialist diagnosis.

The repository does **not** presently allow an independent reader to reproduce from native hospital records alone: the exact 2017 Complex Case Meeting attendance/deliberation and authority chain; the reported protest; native discharge-version chronology; PAR evidential architecture; SIMT deliberative architecture; or Article 18 operational handling.
