## Why these are supplementary rather than minimum-pack documents

The 2018, 2023 and 2025 records are sufficient to reproduce persistence, later OFF/dystonia questions, explicit misalignment and later dopaminergic modification. The 2019, 2020 and 2022 records strengthen continuity and preserve important contrary detail, but adding them to the minimum pack would increase volume without changing the basic evidential structure.
