# Ongoing Patient Statement Log — Repository Notice

The repository contains an ongoing first-person source log at:

`patient-source/PATIENT_STATEMENT_LOG.md`

## Repository rule

This log is a living source collection. Liam may add comments or recollections from time to time.

Future case work should be aware of the log and consult it where first-person evidence is materially relevant, particularly for:

- order of events personally experienced;
- words spoken directly to Liam;
- Liam's own contemporaneous words and intended meaning;
- the reported bedside protest;
- witness presence personally observed;
- Podiatry treatment actually received;
- treatment effects personally experienced where documentary records are incomplete.

The log is **not** a substitute for the documentary record and should not be used to expand patient voice unnecessarily. The case remains document-led.

Entries are source material first. They should not be promoted into propositions or public narrative without comparison against the documentary record and, where available, independent corroboration or contradiction.

When a new patient statement materially changes or clarifies a live proposition, the appropriate workflow is:

**PATIENT STATEMENT LOG → source comparison → evidential assessment → proposition update if justified → public prose if justified.**

Do not retrospectively edit an earlier statement to make it agree with later evidence. Add a new dated clarification instead, preserving the history of Liam's own account.
