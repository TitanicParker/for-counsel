# For Counsel

## Purpose

This repository is a counsel-facing case instrument concerning diagnostic governance, corrigibility, reliance, and record accuracy in a young-onset movement-disorder case.

It is not a memoir, campaign page, or general medical narrative. Its purpose is to organise the case so that counsel can rapidly understand the legal and evidential structure without having to reconstruct it from a large clinical record.

The central proposition is narrow:

> This is not a hindsight misdiagnosis case. It is a corrigibility and reliance case. The issue is not that medicine began in uncertainty; it is that medicine recorded uncertainty and then refused to let uncertainty govern.

## Core case theory

The patient entered the system through a foot-centred gait failure. The GP referral preserved diagnostic openness and sought urgent neurological assessment of abnormal gait, bilateral foot problems, stiffness, increased tone, impaired movement initiation, dexterity decline, and work-threatening deterioration.

The hospital then adopted a Parkinsonian frame. That frame was not baseless. The record contained real parkinsonian signs and later DAT imaging supported a presynaptic dopaminergic disorder. The legal issue is different: whether plausibility was allowed to become authority before the initiating foot-centred problem had been resolved, falsified, or expressly superseded.

The 2017 record did not lack uncertainty. It recorded young onset, atypicality, possible genetic Parkinsonism, dystonic-looking gait, preserved cognition, medication caution, deferred dopaminergic therapy, and unresolved foot symptoms. The problem is that these uncertainties were preserved as text while being deprived of operative force.

The patient was not simply ignored. He was recorded, quoted, counselled, copied into correspondence, and treated as intelligent. But documentation is not corrigibility. A symptom can be present in the record and still be denied governing power.

## Working legal frame

The possible legal principle for counsel to test is:

> In a complex diagnostic case, especially where the patient is young, atypical, and the record itself preserves unresolved features, a healthcare institution must not allow a provisional diagnostic frame to acquire durable clinical, statutory, administrative, or interpretive authority without preserving a meaningful route for correction, qualification, or reopening.

This framing avoids the weaker allegation that later genetic confirmation should be imposed retrospectively on 2017 clinicians. The allegation is that the 2017 record already knew enough to keep the frame revisable.

## Rules for handling source material

1. Redacted clinical source documents must remain separate from analysis.
2. Source pages should state that no analytical findings are made on that page.
3. Interpretive pages may quote source documents sparingly, but should link back to the full redacted source page.
4. Claims should be framed as propositions for counsel, not as final legal determinations.
5. The site should not over-claim. Its force lies in sequence, reliance, and corrigibility.

## Governing sentence

The record did not merely remember the patient; it governed him. The patient's labour was the labour of making the record answerable to the body it had classified.
