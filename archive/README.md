# Historical analytical material

Earlier site pages developed broader “corrigibility”, “premature finality”, “frame management” and related theories. They are not silently deleted by the present revision.

For counsel-facing purposes, the controlling page is now the repository [`README.md`](../README.md), which narrows the case to the source-provenanced documentary hinge and expressly states what is established, disputed, unknown, expert-dependent and disclosure-dependent.

The earlier pages remain part of repository history and may still be useful as project history or issue generation, but they should not be cited as primary evidence or treated as the current legal formulation.

### Superseded framing now qualified

The new overview does not present the following as established propositions:

- that “plausibility became authority too soon” is itself a legal breach;
- that uncertainty was deliberately “deprived of operative force”;
- that later contradiction was institutionally “contained” as a finding;
- that the record “governed” the patient as a legal proposition;
- that later medication response proves the feet were neurological in 2017;
- that SIMT failed to revalidate the historical hinge;
- that Article 18 was unlawfully ignored;
- that an incomplete record automatically postpones limitation;
- that fraud, cover-up, collusion, concealment or dishonesty is established.

Those formulations may remain useful as historical analytical prompts only where converted back into a precise, source-supported factual or legal proposition.
