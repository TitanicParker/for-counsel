# Investigative Posture

**Branch:** `agent/counsel-site-rotation`  
**Function:** governing voice and working forensic posture for the second-generation counsel rewrite.

This file is not a substitute for the fact register or source genealogy. Its purpose is to stop the rewrite from reverting to a detached, over-defensive summary of propositions that the project has already established at the working evidential level.

## 1. Start from the case state already reached

The rewrite is not beginning from zero.

The 2017 hinge record is materially impeached as a complete, self-sufficient account of the transition by which the unresolved bilateral foot/lower-limb problem moved into an operative podiatry pathway. The rewrite should therefore investigate forward from that state rather than repeatedly asking whether the record may be incomplete.

The controlling question is not whether severe Parkinsonism existed. It did. The question is what happened when an early Parkinsonian management frame became controlling before the painful-foot/lower-limb phenotype, aetiology and treatment alternatives had been fully resolved, and what happened to that unresolved state over the next eight years.

## 2. Work at three scales

### Hours — first lock

The first assessment and the levodopa decision appear to have been separated by roughly fourteen hours. On the present reconstruction, the senior/departmental position to withhold levodopa crystallised before the full painful-foot phenotype had been investigated and before the later mature PRKN/foot analysis was assembled.

That matters because the levodopa decision was not merely a treatment preference. Once treated as settled, it constrained the most obvious pharmacological route by which an unresolved painful, dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenon might have been tested for dopaminergic responsiveness during the admission.

The working question is therefore:

> **Did an early treatment decision begin to determine how the still-emerging phenotype was classified, rather than the completed phenotype determining the treatment strategy?**

This is the first lock.

### Days/weeks — second lock

The second lock is documentary and administrative.

Parkinson's disease became sufficiently operative to govern treatment, work advice, LTI, discharge planning, referrals and follow-up while substantial uncertainty remained inside the narrative. The LTI was processed early. The final discharge artefact then accumulated later material, including developed PRKN reasoning and a post-discharge DaTSCAN, around the earlier governing frame.

The DaTSCAN is real evidence of a dopaminergic/parkinsonian disorder. But it was later evidence. When folded into the completed discharge document it could give the earlier decision sequence greater retrospective coherence and authority than the contemporaneous sequence itself had.

The painful feet/lower limbs did not fit neatly inside that framework. Dystonic-looking gait and PRKN were present in the same clinical environment, but the document does not integrate them with the pain, burning and foot problems. Instead, the feet enter a parallel podiatry/peripheral lane while their neurological relationship remains expressly `Not clear`.

Taken as a whole, the working structural hypothesis remains:

> **uncertainty remained clinically while certainty governed administratively.**

### Years — inherited classification

The case does not end in July 2017.

The second lock matters because it creates an inherited record state. Over the following eight years the feet repeatedly return inside Neurology: medication-sensitive phenomena, cramps, abnormal lower-limb movement, toe curling, OFF/dystonia reasoning, explicit 2023 `misalignment`, severe morning foot pain, deliberate Sinemet CR treatment directed at the morning experience, substantial relief, and later PRKN confirmation of Parkinsonism and dystonia.

The longitudinal question is not simply how many later opportunities were missed. It is:

> **What happens when an unresolved clinical question is converted into an apparently settled administrative classification and that classification becomes the inherited starting point for later care and later institutional review?**

## 3. The protest belongs at the centre of the hinge

Liam's working account is that he protested at the bedside transition and that four medical students were present.

That account is to be treated as a serious affirmative evidential proposition, not as a disposable footnote and not as something rebutted merely by silence in the completed discharge record. The record whose silence is being relied upon is itself materially impeached at the hinge.

The protest matters because it sits at the point where both locks become self-protecting. If it occurred as described, it would mean that the early treatment/pathway decision was being challenged contemporaneously and that the completed record did not preserve a material counter-state while preserving collective language and `good understanding`.

The four students therefore form a defined potential witness class.

The live forensic question is:

> **Who were the four students present at the bedside protest, can they be identified from teaching/placement/ward-round records, and what does each independently remember before being exposed to Liam's later reconstruction?**

The strongest evidence would be independent recognition and unprompted recollection of presence, disagreement, subject matter, words used, or clinician response. Even partial independent recollection could materially change the evidential status of the protest and the completeness analysis of the discharge record.

## 4. The double-lock model

The working model should be expressed plainly:

1. **Clinical/therapeutic lock:** an early senior/departmental decision to withhold levodopa becomes governing before the full painful-foot phenotype has been investigated.
2. **Administrative/documentary lock:** Parkinson's/LTI/discharge architecture formalises the pathway; later DaTSCAN and developed reasoning are incorporated into the completed record; the reported protest is not preserved in that completed account.
3. **Longitudinal persistence:** later evidence enters a system already organised by that inherited classification and must overcome not merely an old clinical opinion but a completed institutional history.

The important inference to test is not motive. It is mechanism:

> **the later document may have retrospectively supplied coherence to the earlier decision, and the inherited record may then have stabilised that decision over time.**

## 5. Voice of the rewrite

The second-generation site should sound like a real investigation of a real clinical history.

It should:

- state the working evidential position already reached instead of repeatedly retreating to generic caveats;
- distinguish hours, days/weeks and years so the reader can see crystallisation, documentary consolidation and downstream inheritance;
- ask what each actor actually knew at the moment of decision, not what the final document later contained;
- treat absence in an impeached record as weak evidence where the disputed proposition concerns the very hinge at which the record is incomplete;
- follow information flows: who knew about the foot pain, who knew about dystonic-looking gait, who knew about PRKN, who advised on levodopa, what reached Healy, what reached Counihan, and what entered the final record;
- keep the eight-year longitudinal consequences visible rather than collapsing the case into Monday/Tuesday 2017;
- use later treatment response and genetics to illuminate reopening and path dependency, not as automatic hindsight proof;
- put the patient's reported protest and the four-student witness question into the main investigative architecture rather than isolating them as an evidential embarrassment;
- preserve contrary evidence without allowing it to erase the mechanism under investigation.

The governing sentence for the rewrite is:

> **A provisional early Parkinson's disease framework became therapeutically and administratively controlling before the painful-foot phenotype, aetiology and treatment alternatives had been fully resolved. The completed discharge record then consolidated later information around that early framework, while not preserving the patient's reported protest, producing an institutional history that appears more integrated, consensual and retrospectively confirmed than the underlying decision sequence can presently be shown to have been.**

This is the posture from which the new counsel pages should be written and the proposition map should be built.
