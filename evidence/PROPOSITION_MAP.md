# Controlling Proposition Map

**Status:** working bridge between F0001–F0283 and counsel-facing prose  
**Branch:** `agent/counsel-site-rotation`  
**Rule:** no proposition in this file is a pleading or final expert conclusion merely because it is listed here.

The second-generation counsel presentation will not ask readers to work directly from 283 atomic facts. This map identifies the smaller number of propositions that can carry the narrative while remaining traceable to the fact register and source library.

Each proposition is classified as one of:

- **ESTABLISHED DOCUMENTARY PROPOSITION** — the source architecture presently supports the proposition at the stated level;
- **SOURCE-DISCIPLINED INFERENCE** — a synthesis from multiple documentary facts that remains open to qualification;
- **EXPERT QUESTION** — requires independent medical opinion;
- **LEGAL QUESTION** — requires counsel;
- **SOURCE-DEPENDENT / OPEN** — controlling material remains missing.

## P02 — Severe Parkinsonism was genuinely identified

**Status:** ESTABLISHED DOCUMENTARY PROPOSITION

## P09 — At least part of the foot/lower-limb phenomenon showed early neurological pharmacological modifiability

**Status:** SOURCE-DISCIPLINED INFERENCE; mechanism remains EXPERT QUESTION

# Questions generated by the proposition map

The proposition map is intended to lead naturally to a small set of genuine questions rather than predetermined answers:

1. What medically coherent explanation accounts for repeated neurological treatment-state changes involving the foot/lower-limb syndrome in a patient later shown to have PRKN-caused Parkinsonism and dystonia, while mechanical/local/neuropathic features also existed?
2. At what point, if any, should those later signals have caused the original `Not clear → podiatry` classification to be revisited?
3. Was the 2017 levodopa strategy reasonable in the circumstances, and what diagnostic/review safeguards should accompany such a strategy?
4. What exactly was communicated to and understood by Liam about diagnosis, uncertainty, treatment deferral, podiatry and later options?
5. What did the Complex Case Meeting, Prof Counihan and Prof Healy actually contribute, as distinct from later descriptions of their involvement?
6. What was the exact version-state chronology of the discharge artefact and LTI process?
7. What did Podiatry receive, conclude, treat and communicate back?
8. What evidence did the PAR/SIMT process actually test before reaching the no-deficit conclusion?
9. What damage, if any, is attributable to the disputed classification/treatment pathway rather than underlying disease progression?
10. What is the correct claim-specific limitation analysis given the March 2025 knowledge evidence and later discoveries?

# Writing instruction for the counsel site

The public-facing site should not expose this file as a substitute for prose.

The writer should use it as a control layer:

**fact IDs support propositions; propositions support paragraphs; paragraphs lead the reader through the chronology.**

Where a proposition is an inference, expert question or source-dependent proposition, the prose must say so naturally rather than laundering it into factual certainty.
