## Testable question

For each row, the controlling question is:

> **Did the new information merely change treatment locally, or is there an identified document showing that it changed the historical classification, responsibility allocation or governing premise that the new information called into question?**

A blank or negative answer is evidence of absence from the identified record, not proof that no clinical reconsideration ever occurred. Where that distinction matters, disclosure and expert evidence remain necessary.
