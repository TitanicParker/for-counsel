## 1. Healy's September 2017 specialist opinion is now directly available

A photographed letter dated 12 September 2017 from Prof Daniel G. Healy to Dr Thomas Monaghan has now been supplied and inspected.

Healy records:

> “Thank you for asking me to see this man who I think probably has Park II and I would suggest testing that initially. If it is negative I would go on to test a panel.”

He also records:

> “It is a little bit unclear but he probably developed Parkinsonism somewhere between his late 20s and early 30s.”

and:

> “Physical activity would be helpful and as you are aware patients with PARK II tend to manage quite well for long periods of time with relatively low doses of Dopamine replacement therapy. They can become quite dyskinetic if over-used. One should mentally think of it as a nigropathy.”

He records that he would be interested in the test results and would be happy to see Liam again if required.

### Documentary consequence

The prior source gap described as `native Healy reply missing` is closed at the level of substantive content. The original image remains the controlling source and should be preserved in native/redacted form where appropriate.

The letter directly establishes that the external specialist did more than merely entertain a generic genetic differential: after seeing Liam, Healy recorded that he **probably had PARK II** and recommended testing that first.

It also distinguishes relatively low-dose dopamine replacement from over-use/dyskinesia risk. It does not expressly direct immediate levodopa commencement. The question of how Galway received, interpreted and acted upon the advice remains open.
