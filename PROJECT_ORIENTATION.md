# Project Evidential Orientation

**Repository:** `TitanicParker/for-counsel`  
**Active evidential branch:** `agent/source-extraction-genealogy`  
**Status:** source-led navigation document for counsel / independent movement-disorders review.  
**Function:** orientation, source-acquisition control and argument-refinement guide. It is not pleading and it is not a replacement for the underlying records.  
**Current rule:** where this README states a conclusion, follow the links to the primary record and test it independently.

The project has one recurring purpose: **make the strongest source-disciplined argument easier and easier to see.** That happens by finding primary material, preserving it, classifying it, tracing its genealogy, testing the proposition it bears upon, refining the argument, and then identifying the next source capable of improving or changing the analysis.

The strongest present case is **not** that Parkinsonism was invented, that every foot symptom was dystonia, or that later medication response automatically proves negligence. Strong contemporaneous evidence supports a real parkinsonian disorder. The narrower documentary problem concerns the classification and treatment history of the disabling foot/lower-limb phenomenon: the 2017 record expressly retained uncertainty about its neurological relationship, podiatry became an operative pathway, and later records contain repeated evidence that at least part of the foot phenomenon was neurologically pharmacologically modifiable.

---

## Evidential labels used here

- **PRIMARY FACT** — directly recorded event, observation, test, treatment or contemporaneous communication.
- **PATIENT ACCOUNT** — what Liam contemporaneously or later reported; evidence of the account and, where contemporaneous, of his knowledge/state of mind.
- **CLINICAL OPINION** — clinician interpretation or diagnosis, distinguished from raw observation.
- **INFERENCE** — conclusion drawn by comparing sources; not itself a primary fact.
- **ADVERSE / QUALIFYING** — evidence that narrows or may weaken the strongest case and must remain visible.
- **EXPERT QUESTION** — requires independent clinical interpretation before any medico-legal conclusion.
- **UNKNOWN / SOURCE REQUIRED** — not established from the present permanent source corpus.

## 1. The governing reliability boundary

**INFERENCE.** The 2017 record remains strong evidence of observed neurological signs, severe parkinsonism, recorded clinical reasoning, treatment decisions, the fact of podiatry referral, LTI/DaTSCAN chronology and later follow-up. It is **not safely self-sufficient proof** that the unresolved relationship between the bilateral foot/lower-limb problem and the neurological syndrome was actually resolved as separate before or through the podiatry pathway, or that podiatry disposition itself proves neurological closure.

This is proposition-specific impeachment, not a claim that the whole medical record is unreliable.

The working question is therefore not “Was Parkinson's wrong?” It is:

> **What happened to the unresolved disabling foot/lower-limb phenomenon after the record said its relationship to the neurological syndrome was “Not clear”, and what should later treatment-response and dystonia evidence have done to that classification?**

## 3. Early neurological pharmacological modifiability

**PRIMARY FACT.** Kemadrin/Procyclidine was prescribed as neurological symptomatic treatment in 2017, alongside — not instead of — the separate podiatry plan.

**PRIMARY LATER CLINICAL RECORD OF PATIENT-REPORTED RESPONSE.** The April 2018 Neurology record preserves the short Sept/Oct 2017 withdrawal history. When Procyclidine was stopped because of visual blurring, the record says the first thing changing, and changing rapidly, was the return/change at the outside-left-foot/corn site; symptoms worsened somewhat and Liam was more “shifty” and “unrelaxed” off the drug.

**PATIENT ACCOUNT / PRIMARY CONTEMPORANEOUS COMMUNICATION.** A 24 October 2017 Gmail message (`Re: Medical Assessment`, message ID `15f4eacd53c5073c`) independently records approximately 12 days off the medication, restart at a lower dose, incomplete symptom coverage at the reduced dose, and a planned increase. Liam described himself as **“physically very much improved”** and normal movement/functioning as **“living in a miracle.”**

**INFERENCE.** These sources together are evidence that at least part of the foot phenomenon was neurologically pharmacologically modifiable in the early treatment period. They do **not** establish that the corn itself was neurological, that all bilateral foot pain was dystonia, or that podiatry was inappropriate.

**SOURCE STATUS.** The Gmail source is known and directly examined but is not yet mirrored as a permanent repository source artefact.

## 4. Longitudinal neurological ownership of the foot problem

The foot problem does not disappear after the 2017 podiatry disposition. It repeatedly returns inside the same Neurology pathway.

- **2018 — PRIMARY FACT:** foot cramps continue; toe supports help; the earlier Procyclidine withdrawal signal is documented; Neurology records that levodopa/dopamine agonist treatment could make Liam substantially better while explaining why it was being deferred.
- **2020 — PRIMARY FACT / CLINICAL OPINION:** exquisite foot sensitivity, excessive movement/urge involving the feet, and a left lower-limb “throw” characterised as parkinsonian are recorded.
- **2021 — PRIMARY FACT:** Sinemet is introduced and escalated while treatment-risk/dyskinesia concerns remain active.
- **2022 — PRIMARY FACT:** the feet remain sore and take roughly 20–25 minutes to “warm up” in the morning.
- **2023 — PRIMARY FACT / CLINICAL OPINION:** severe bilateral foot pain, possible OFF-dystonia material, dopaminergic adjustment and explicit clinician-patient “misalignment” over the explanatory relationship of Parkinsonism and the feet appear in the record; the history also recognises that the earliest symptoms were in the feet.
- **2024 — PRIMARY FACT / ADVERSE-QUALIFYING:** foot pain and toe curling persist; pregabalin/Lyrica benefit supports the possibility of mixed neurological/neuropathic or pain-modulation mechanisms rather than one simple explanation.

This continuity matters because the foot phenomenon was not simply handed to podiatry and then lost to Neurology. It remained a recurring neurological management problem over years.

## 5. What the patient was being told: 2017 and 2021

**PRIMARY FACT.** The 2017 record's “good understanding” wording belongs to the surrounding explanation of treatment strategy/levodopa deferral. It should not be expanded into proof that Liam globally agreed the feet were separate or that podiatry had resolved the presenting problem.

**PRIMARY FACT.** The signed October 2021 Neurology letter says:

> “the DBS is only ever for the tremor element. It will not help with anything else.”

The same letter says Liam had been fully counselled and would receive a copy at home.

**EXTERNAL MEDICAL-AUTHORITY POINT ALREADY VERIFIED IN PROJECT WORK.** By 2021, DBS was not confined as a general treatment proposition to tremor; established guidance recognised DBS uses in Parkinson's disease beyond tremor and in selected dystonia. A narrower patient-specific judgment — that DBS would only materially benefit Liam's tremor at that stage — may have been intended, but that is **not the wording the letter records**.

**EXPERT QUESTION.** Nothing here establishes that Liam should have undergone DBS in 2021. An independent movement-disorders expert must determine his actual phenotype, treatment state, DBS suitability and whether the categorical counselling had any clinical significance.

**EVIDENTIAL RELEVANCE.** The letter is relevant to the information frame actually supplied to an engaged patient who was expected to rely on specialist counselling. It may bear on what Liam could reasonably be expected to understand without independently correcting his neurologist's account of treatment possibilities.

## 6. March 2025: the foot problem is deliberately treated through dopaminergic coverage

**PRIMARY FACT / RECORDED PATIENT ACCOUNT.** By March 2025 the feet were again the dominant clinical problem. The note records severe morning foot pain, inability initially to tolerate normal steps, and that once Parkinson's medication reached his system the feet felt better from a pain perspective.

**CLINICAL OPINION / PRIMARY FACT OF TREATMENT.** The clinician considered that additional Sinemet might provide further relief but weighed dyskinesia risk. Long-acting Sinemet CR was then introduced at night specifically in response to the severe morning foot problem / possible overnight under-treatment.

This means the later foot response was not merely an accidental observation following treatment prescribed for an unrelated symptom.

**ADVERSE / QUALIFYING.** The same record does not treat all pain as straightforward Parkinsonian pain. It preserves peripheral/mixed possibilities, pregabalin benefit, painful dystonia as one possible category, and dyskinesia risk from increasing dopaminergic treatment.

## 10. What the present evidence supports — and does not support

### Supported on the present source corpus

- **PRIMARY FACT:** genuine severe parkinsonism was present in 2017.
- **PRIMARY FACT / CLINICAL OPINION:** dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenology was recorded contemporaneously.
- **PRIMARY FACT:** the foot-neurology relationship was expressly left “Not clear.”
- **PRIMARY FACT:** podiatry became an operative foot pathway.
- **INFERENCE:** the presently accessible 2017 sources do not demonstrate the reasoning bridge by which uncertainty became proof of separation/resolution.
- **INFERENCE supported by separated source events:** at least part of the longstanding foot phenomenon showed neurological pharmacological modifiability in the early Procyclidine period and again under levodopa/Sinemet in 2025.
- **PRIMARY FACT / CLINICAL OPINION:** later records contain OFF/dystonia questions, continuing foot morbidity and deliberate dopaminergic management of the severe foot problem.
- **SPECIALIST DIAGNOSIS:** 2026 genetics attributes both Parkinsonism and dystonia to compound heterozygous Parkin/PRKN mutations.

### Not established merely by those facts

- that every historical foot symptom had one dystonic cause;
- that every corn/callus was neurological;
- that podiatry was clinically inappropriate or negligent;
- that levodopa should necessarily have been commenced in 2017;
- that DBS should have been performed in 2021;
- that later medication response automatically proves a 2017 breach;
- that later genetics can be back-projected into knowledge available in 2017;
- that any clinician acted dishonestly, unfairly, fraudulently or in bad faith.

## 11. Strongest adverse and benign explanations to preserve

A competent review must keep these possibilities visible:

- the 2017 syndrome was genuinely parkinsonian and later DaTSCAN evidence supported a presynaptic dopaminergic disorder;
- the feet may have contained **mixed** mechanical/corn/callus, neuropathic, pain-modulation and dystonic/neurological components;
- podiatry may have been reasonable **adjunctive** treatment even if the neurological relationship remained unresolved;
- caution with levodopa/dopaminergic escalation in a very young patient may have been clinically defensible, particularly given dyskinesia/impulse-control concerns;
- Lyrica/pregabalin later helped pain, supporting non-unitary mechanisms;
- the 2021 DBS wording may have been imprecise shorthand for a sound patient-specific judgment not fully expressed in the letter;
- an unseen PAR or other missing primary material could contain genuine independent revalidation of disputed historical propositions;
- the reported bedside protest remains uncorroborated patient evidence unless further source material is found.

## 13. The movement-disorders questions

The present documentary record should be put to an independent expert without assuming the disputed hinge is resolved.

**EXPERT QUESTIONS:**

1. What medically coherent explanation best accounts for a longstanding foot/lower-limb phenomenon that changed with Procyclidine withdrawal in 2017, remained clinically important during Neurology follow-up, and later improved when dopaminergic coverage was deliberately altered in 2025?
2. What portions, if any, are best understood as dystonia/OFF phenomena, pain modulation, abnormal mechanical loading secondary to movement disorder, neuropathy/local podiatric pathology, or a mixed state?
3. What clinical significance should have been attached to the early withdrawal-response evidence?
4. At what point, if any, should evolving treatment-response and phenomenological evidence have caused the original unresolved foot/podiatry classification to be reconsidered?
5. What, if anything, did the 2021 phenotype imply for DBS counselling or referral, distinct from whether DBS was actually indicated?
6. What does the later PRKN result legitimately illuminate retrospectively, and what would constitute impermissible hindsight?

These are expert questions. The documentary record does not answer breach or causation by itself.

- **other source formats** as they are identified.

### Source-materialisation rule

For each important source:

1. **locate the original or best available first-hand artefact;**
2. **preserve it exactly before editing/redaction;**
3. record its **source location, date, author, recipient, filename, format and provenance**;
4. distinguish the original from any later redacted/publication copy;
5. classify what the source actually proves and what it does not prove;
6. identify whether it is an independent evidential origin or a repetition/derivative use;
7. place or reference a stable, appropriately redacted source artefact in the permanent repository where lawful and appropriate;
8. update the source inventory/genealogy; and
9. **re-test the argument against the source before changing the case presentation.**

Do not duplicate evidence merely to make the repository look complete. Preserve source identity and lineage.

## 17. The recursive project method — the governing activity

The project is not a static archive and it is not a sequence of ever-longer theories. Its recurring activity is:

> **FIND SOURCE → PRESERVE SOURCE → CLASSIFY SOURCE → TRACE GENEALOGY → TEST PROPOSITION → REFINE ARGUMENT → MAKE IT EASIER TO SEE → IDENTIFY NEXT SOURCE → REPEAT.**

Every new primary source must be allowed to strengthen, narrow, qualify, displace or defeat an existing proposition.

The test for each iteration is not “have we added more material?” It is:

> **Is the strongest defensible argument now clearer, shorter, better sourced and easier for an independent lawyer or expert to reconstruct?**

The eventual successor to the present counsel-facing case should emerge from this recursive process. It should be better than the current `for-counsel` presentation because the source architecture makes the argument more obvious, not because uncertainty has been edited away.
