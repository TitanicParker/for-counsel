# Patient Statement Log

**Status:** ongoing first-person source log  
**Source origin:** Liam Moloney  
**Purpose:** preserve patient-origin evidence supplied over time without forcing it immediately into the case theory, proposition map, or public narrative.

## Governing rule

This is a **source log**, not an analytical document.

Entries should preserve Liam's own account as closely as possible and should not be silently harmonised with the institutional record or later case theory.

The repository should consult this log only where patient evidence is materially relevant. The case remains document-led.

Patient voice is especially relevant to:

- the order of events Liam personally experienced;
- what clinicians directly said to him;
- what he said in response;
- the reported bedside protest;
- who was present at encounters Liam personally witnessed;
- what he meant by his own recorded distinctions;
- the Podiatry treatment he actually received;
- what treatment effects he personally experienced where the records do not fully capture them.

The log should **not** be used merely to duplicate facts better established by contemporaneous records.
