Adverse and qualifying evidence

What cuts the other way

The case is stronger when counsel can see, immediately and without excavation, the evidence that may narrow it.

Parkinsonism was real and severe

The early neurological examination recorded marked symmetrical rigidity, gross bradykinesia and a profoundly abnormal gait. The later DaTSCAN was abnormal and supported a presynaptic dopaminergic disorder. The later PRKN result does not undo those findings; it supplies a genetic explanation for Parkinsonism and dystonia.

The levodopa strategy had a recorded rationale

The record repeatedly explains concern about exposing a very young person to dyskinesia, impulse-control disorder and dopamine dysregulation over a long treatment horizon. The fact that levodopa may have helped does not itself prove that deferring it was unreasonable. Standard of care requires independent movement-disorders evidence.

There were genuine local and non-dopaminergic foot features

Pes planus, hammertoes, corns and calluses were documented. Toe supports helped. Pregabalin/Lyrica later substantially reduced pain. These facts are inconsistent with a simplistic theory that every foot symptom was a single dopamine-responsive dystonia.

The medication-response history is not uniformly clean

The short 2017 Procyclidine withdrawal provides a strong early treatment-state association, but later Procyclidine withdrawal was more ambiguous. In 2023 Liam also stopped Parkinson medication for approximately ten days and reported no difference “from a Parkinson’s perspective” while severe bilateral foot and leg pain persisted. That evidence qualifies any claim that the whole painful syndrome tracked dopamine in a simple way.

The clinician repeatedly preserved alternative explanations

The record considered peripheral neuropathy, structural foot pathology, Fabry disease and pain amplification in Parkinsonian OFF states. In March 2025 the neurologist expressly stated that the pain described was not, in his view, direct Parkinson’s phenomenology, even while acknowledging that dystonia can be painful and that more Sinemet would probably provide additional relief.

Podiatry may have been a reasonable adjunct

The fact that the neurological relationship was “Not clear” does not make podiatry inappropriate. A patient can have neurological disease and mechanical foot pathology at the same time. The outstanding Podiatry disclosure is therefore capable of weakening, strengthening or materially revising the present hinge analysis.

The patient was extensively documented and counselled

The historical record is unusually detailed. The clinician copied Liam into correspondence, explained treatment decisions and later said those long letters were intended to preserve shared understanding. The narrow “good understanding” entry regarding levodopa deferral is evidence that must be taken seriously, even though it does not establish agreement with every diagnosis or foot classification.

The hospital later found no deficit in care

The SIMT outcome records a multidisciplinary conclusion that the care was appropriate and that there was “no deficit in the care provided”. That conclusion is adverse evidence. The present source limitation is that the complete PAR and SIMT source architecture are not available, so the exact propositions tested cannot yet be reconstructed.

March 2025 is important adverse limitation evidence

By 19 March 2025 Liam was formally alleging diagnostic anchoring, treatment delay and historical harm. Later discoveries may provide new evidence or new causal coherence, but they do not automatically postpone a legal date of knowledge. Limitation is claim-specific and belongs with counsel.

The later genetic result is not a retrospective standard-of-care answer

The 2026 Parkin result confirms the eventual genetic diagnosis and attributes Parkinsonism and dystonia to the mutations. It cannot be used as though it were information available in 2017, nor does it establish that every historical foot symptom had the same mechanism.

Why this page exists. The proposed case should survive contact with its strongest contrary evidence. If a proposition cannot survive that test, it should not appear in the counsel-facing narrative.
