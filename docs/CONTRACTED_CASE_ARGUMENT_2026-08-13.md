## Impeachment pre-filter

The project does not treat the entire chart as false or useless.

Objective tests, prescriptions, recorded examinations, dates, direct observations and independently originated evidence retain weight according to provenance.

But where a disputed proposition derives materially from clinician-controlled or institutionally inherited narrative concerning:

- patient agreement or acceptance;
- completeness of disclosure or deliberation;
- decision authority / authorship;
- treatment purpose;
- causal allocation of the feet;
- reassessment;
- historical legitimacy;

it should not be treated as established merely because it appears in the medical record.

It must first be tested for provenance, source genealogy, contemporaneous consistency, contrary evidence and genuinely independent support.

Repetition from a common originating source is not independent corroboration.

The working evidence classes are:

- **Documentary fact** — literal source content.
- **Historical fact** — occurrence reliably established.
- **Patient evidence/allegation** — patient-source proposition assessed on its own support.
- **Clinician representation** — proves that the clinician said/recorded X, not automatically that X happened.
- **Institutional conclusion** — proves that a body reached a decision, not automatically independent historical validation.
- **Strong inference** — convergent source-supported conclusion.
- **Controlling evidence gap** — proposition not presently resolvable.

## What the papers do not prove by themselves

They do not automatically prove:

- that `good understanding` meant agreement or informed participation;
- that Podiatry was accepted as sufficient resolution;
- that peripheral neuropathy was independently established as the cause of the longitudinal foot syndrome;
- that Healy validated maximum/prolonged withholding;
- that Counihan had no material role in formation of the treatment position;
- that probable/suspected PARK2 was validly displaced by settled idiopathic aetiology;
- that pregabalin was originally introduced as a Sinemet-sparing strategy;
- that institutional repetition independently validates the originating clinician narrative;
- that a later formal dystonia diagnosis had no retrospective evidential significance.

## What full impeachment means here

> **Strip the clinician-controlled narrative of automatic evidential privilege where its own completeness, neutrality, authorship, causal allocation or retrospective accuracy is materially disputed. Then use the surviving primary-source facts and adverse admissions affirmatively.**

The project does not need to prove every note false. It needs to identify which propositions the record has forfeited the right to prove by itself.
