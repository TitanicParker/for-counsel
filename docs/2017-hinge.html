The hinge: the patient was still being understood when the framework became hard to change

The important chronology is not simply an eight-day admission. It is the order in which a still-emerging phenotype met an early treatment decision, an administrative diagnosis and a completed record that later made the sequence look more settled than it was.

The case arrived through the feet as well as the gait

The June 2017 GP referral described a young man whose gait, movement and dexterity had deteriorated enough to threaten work. Both feet were materially within the presenting problem: corns, calluses, bilateral foot difficulty and an abnormal gait sat alongside stiffness, increased tone, brisk reflexes and trouble initiating movement. The referral did not deliver a settled diagnosis. It delivered an open neurological problem.

The feet therefore cannot be treated as a later complaint attached to an otherwise complete Parkinson's case. They were inside the problem the hospital was asked to solve.

Severe Parkinsonism was recognised quickly

The neurological syndrome itself was striking. The inpatient examination records markedly increased symmetrical tone, gross bradykinesia and a profoundly abnormal gait. That part of the case was not subtle.

But the lower-limb picture was not ordinary either. The record describes a footdrop-like appearance as somewhat suggestive of a dystonic posture and expressly says “NOT FOOT DROP”. Structural abnormalities, burning, corns and calluses were also present. The problem was therefore not whether Parkinsonism existed. It was how the painful and abnormal lower-limb phenotype fitted into it.

The first lock appears to have formed within roughly fourteen hours

The present decision-state reconstruction places the first substantive neurological assessment on Monday afternoon and the departmental levodopa position by the following Tuesday morning. The exact clock times remain to be fixed from native records, but the clinically important point is the compressed interval: roughly fourteen hours between first assessment and the decision that levodopa would not be used.

On the working reconstruction, that decision crystallised before Monaghan's later, fuller investigation of the painful-foot problem had matured and before the finished record's developed PRKN/foot analysis existed in the form in which it now appears.

This makes the levodopa decision more than a later therapeutic preference. It may have become an early governing condition. Once the department had decided to hold levodopa back, the most obvious pharmacological route for asking whether the unresolved painful, dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenon was dopamine-responsive was no longer available during the admission.

First lock — clinical/therapeutic. The live question is whether an early treatment decision began to determine how the still-emerging phenotype was classified, rather than the completed phenotype determining the treatment strategy.

The feet were investigated after the treatment frame had begun to harden

The later clinical narrative considers whether the leg and foot symptoms might be separate, peripheral, neuropathic or related in some other way. It also records the unusual lower-limb phenomenology and develops a young-onset genetic differential including PRKN/PARK2.

Yet the answer to the relationship between the feet and the neurological syndrome remained: “Not clear.”

That is not a minor phrase. It shows that the clinical uncertainty survived inside the record even after the operative treatment framework had been selected.

The second lock was administrative and documentary

Parkinson's disease became sufficiently operative to govern treatment, follow-up, work advice, LTI paperwork and discharge planning. Podiatry became the practical route for the feet. The LTI state was established before the later DaTSCAN result.

The formal DaTSCAN on 13 July was important objective evidence of a presynaptic dopaminergic disorder. But it was later evidence. When that result was incorporated into the completed discharge artefact by addendum, it sat beside the diagnosis, treatment rationale, LTI state, collective language and podiatry plan as though they formed one mature evidential moment.

They did not. The final document is cumulative. It can therefore give the early framework greater retrospective coherence and authority than the contemporaneous decision sequence itself can presently be shown to have had.

Second lock — administrative/documentary. A provisional early Parkinson's framework became an institutional object: diagnosis, treatment deferral, work/LTI consequences, referral pathways and later objective confirmation were consolidated into one completed record.

The painful-foot problem did not resolve inside that framework

Podiatry may have been entirely appropriate for genuine structural pathology. That is not the issue. The issue is what happened to the neurological question once podiatry became operative while the relationship remained “Not clear”.

The record contains the ingredients of a neurological synthesis — very young onset, marked symmetry, dystonic-looking lower-limb posture, an explicit PRKN/PARK2 differential and severe Parkinsonism — but does not show those ingredients being integrated with the painful feet, burning, corns and calluses before the practical pathway split.

The working description is therefore simple: uncertainty remained clinically while certainty governed administratively.

The protest belongs inside the hinge, not outside it

Liam's account is that he protested at the bedside when the feet were being separated from the neurological problem and that four medical students were present. The completed record does not preserve that protest.

That omission matters because the same completed artefact preserves collective language, senior/departmental authority and a statement of “good understanding”. If the protest occurred as described, the bedside state contained a material counter-position that the final documentary state does not show.

The absence of the protest from an already-impeached hinge record is not treated here as proof that no protest occurred. The four students form a defined potential eyewitness class whose identities and independent recollections should be pursued.

Live witness question. Who were the four students present at the bedside encounter, can they be identified from teaching or placement records, and what does each independently remember before being shown Liam's later reconstruction?

Witness-investigation protocol

PRKN was not missed; it was left inside an unresolved architecture

The early record expressly considered PRKN/PARK2. That is why the case is not well described as hindsight discovery of a diagnosis nobody had contemplated. The more difficult question is what the treating network did with that live causal possibility while the lower-limb phenotype remained abnormal and the painful feet remained unexplained.

The later 2026 Parkin result does not rewrite what was known in 2017. It does, however, make the earlier proximity between PRKN reasoning and dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenology clinically consequential to reconstruct.

The hinge matters because it did not stay in 2017

The two locks matter only because the classification then lived on. Over the following eight years the feet repeatedly re-entered Neurology through medication-sensitive phenomena, cramps, abnormal lower-limb movement, toe curling, OFF/dystonia reasoning, the explicit 2023 “misalignment”, severe morning foot pain and finally deliberate dopaminergic treatment directed at the morning foot experience with substantial relief.

The historical question is therefore not merely whether the original decision was right or wrong on Tuesday morning. It is whether an unresolved clinical question became embedded in a completed institutional history and then remained difficult to reopen as contrary or complicating evidence accumulated.

Working model. The case was potentially locked twice: first by an early treatment decision made before the painful-foot phenotype had been fully assembled, and then by an administrative record that consolidated that framework with LTI, referral pathways, later DaTSCAN confirmation and apparent consensus while not preserving the reported protest. The following eight years test what happened when later evidence entered that inherited classification.
