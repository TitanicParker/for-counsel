The Case Evidence Fact Register

Atomic evidence layer

F0001–F0283

The fact register is the documentary substrate beneath the narrative. It is not itself the case theory.

What a fact ID means

Each fact ID records the smallest proposition an identified source supports at the level actually established. A clinical note can establish that a clinician recorded an observation or opinion without making the underlying proposition automatically true. A patient communication establishes what the patient said and understood at that date. A later institutional decision establishes that the decision was made; it does not silently re-prove the historical facts beneath it.

Why the register matters

The second-generation counsel site is designed so that consequential prose can be traced downwards: first to a tested proposition, then to fact IDs, then to the source and its provenance. That allows the surface presentation to remain readable without sacrificing forensic discipline.

Current status

The register is provisionally frozen at F0283. New analytical writing does not create new facts. New IDs should arise only from genuinely new source material, a newly materialised native version, or disclosure that materially changes source genealogy.

Important boundaries

The register does not count repetition as corroboration, does not convert silence into a positive fact, does not back-project later evidence, and does not treat missing paperwork as proof that an event did not occur. Adverse and qualifying facts sit in the same evidential layer as supportive facts.

Working rule: Source → Documentary Fact → Tested Proposition → Prose.
