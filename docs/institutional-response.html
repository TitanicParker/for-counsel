2025–2026

The later review inherited the same historical record

The governance question is not simply whether the hospital reviewed the complaint. It did. The deeper question is what historical evidence entered that review independently of the completed 2017 record and the later clinician reconstruction built from it.

The complaint entered formal governance

After the March 2025 complaint, QPS correspondence moved the matter into formal hospital structures. Later communications refer to the Incident Management Framework, a Preliminary Assessment Report and consideration by the Serious Incident Management Team.

The 13 June communication represented that the complaint would be reviewed independently through the incident-management process. That is an important process statement. It establishes what the hospital said the review architecture would be.

But independence has to be traced through sources, not labels

The original 2017 history was already a cumulative documentary object. It contained diagnosis, treatment rationale, collective language, apparent patient understanding, Podiatry disposition, work/LTI consequences and later DaTSCAN confirmation. The reported bedside protest was not preserved in that completed account.

That creates a source-genealogy problem for any later review. If a PAR or SIMT process principally relied on the completed record and on Monaghan’s retrospective explanation, then the authority of the conclusion could increase without the underlying historical evidence becoming more independent.

2017 record → later clinician reconstruction → PAR → SIMT → later QPS reliance.

The live question is where, if anywhere, new historical evidence entered that chain.

The no-deficit outcome is real evidence

SIMT later recorded that care was appropriate and that there was “no deficit in the care provided.” The outcome referred to the risks of Sinemet in a younger patient, alternative diagnoses and the fact that ordinary Sinemet had been prescribed from 2021.

That conclusion must be carried with the case. The repository is not entitled to erase it simply because it is adverse.

The 2021 Sinemet proposition does not answer the narrower March 2025 question

Ordinary Sinemet did begin in 2021. The March 2025 issue was different: controlled-release Sinemet was deliberately introduced into the severe morning-foot problem, and the later treating account described “profound relief currently”.

The governance question is therefore whether the review separately reconstructed and tested the treatment-response event that changed the patient’s understanding, rather than merely establishing that levodopa had been used at some point before 2025.

The protest creates another independent-reconstruction question

If Liam’s reported bedside protest occurred as described, the 2017 record did not preserve a material contemporaneous disagreement at the very hinge later under challenge. A governance review relying on that record would not discover the protest unless it sought evidence outside the record.

The four medical students therefore matter to governance as well as to the clinical history. Independent corroboration would require the 2017 completeness analysis to be rerun and would also raise a new question: whether later institutional reviewers were evaluating a materially incomplete historical account.

The complete PAR and SIMT architecture remains missing

The repository does not presently hold the complete PAR, its source schedule or attachments, the SIMT agenda/minutes or the full circulation/source pack. Without those materials, it cannot yet answer several critical questions:

  • Was the 2017 foot/Podiatry hinge reconstructed from primary sources or accepted from the inherited record?
  • Was the reported protest known to the review?
  • Was the 2017 Procyclidine withdrawal/rechallenge evidence considered?
  • Was the March 2025 foot-pain/Sinemet CR sequence tested as a distinct proposition?
  • Was the early PRKN/dystonia architecture compared with the later retrospective account?
  • What evidence, if any, came independently from Counihan, Healy, Podiatry or other witnesses?

The correct present formulation

Current evidential position. A formal governance process and a no-deficit outcome are established. Independent proposition-level revalidation of the disputed 2017 foot/Podiatry hinge, protest and later reopening evidence has not yet been demonstrated from the materials presently available.

That is not a conclusion that SIMT failed to investigate. The missing source pack may contain independent work not visible in the outcome letter. Until it is obtained, the source genealogy must remain open.

Why this matters to the whole case

The double-lock model does not stop with clinical care. If the second lock created a completed record that appeared more settled than the underlying sequence, later institutional review could inherit the same appearance of settlement. The key forensic question is whether governance reopened the historical evidence or merely reviewed an inherited documentary state.

This is mechanism, not motive. Institutional authority can accumulate through repetition and review without anyone acting dishonestly. The task is to identify where genuine independent evidential input entered.
