What the institution later reviewed — and what can presently be demonstrated

The governance record should be read as a process chronology, not as proof either that the historical care was vindicated or that the review was inadequate.

The complaint moved into formal hospital processes

After the March 2025 complaint, QPS correspondence records escalation into hospital governance structures. Later correspondence refers to IMF review, a Patient Assessment Report and presentation to the Serious Incident Management Team.

The 13 June QPS communication describes the complaint as having been reviewed independently through the hospital incident-management structure after consultation with the Clinical Director for Medicine. That statement proves what the hospital represented about the process architecture. It does not, by itself, establish what primary evidence was actually tested proposition by proposition.

PAR and SIMT followed

By August 2025 the patient was told through Patient Advocacy that a PAR was scheduled for presentation at the August SIMT. The SIMT later met on 27 August and a written outcome followed.

The outcome records that the multidisciplinary membership considered the management appropriate, refers to risk factors associated with Sinemet in a younger patient, notes that alternative diagnoses were considered, and records that Sinemet was first prescribed in 2021. The conclusion was that there was no deficit in care.

The 2021 Sinemet statement does not answer the narrower March 2025 issue

Ordinary Sinemet did begin in 2021. That proposition is true. The separate March 2025 issue is that controlled-release Sinemet was introduced into the severe morning-foot problem at that time, with the later clinician account recording “profound relief currently”.

The governance question is therefore not whether the SIMT statement about 2021 is false. It is whether the review separately considered the narrower treatment-response proposition that had triggered the complaint.

The present source boundary

The repository does not presently hold the complete PAR, its source schedule or attachments, the SIMT agenda/minutes, or the full circulation/source pack. Without those materials, the project cannot reconstruct which propositions were put before the reviewers, what primary sources were available, or how the 2017 foot/Podiatry hinge and March 2025 response were tested.

The defensible formulation is therefore:

Independent revalidation of the disputed foot/Podiatry hinge has not yet been demonstrated from the materials presently available.

That is not the same as saying the review failed. The missing pack may contain the very analysis presently absent from the disclosed outcome letter.

Data-protection and rectification processes ran alongside the complaint

Article 16, Article 18 and later DPC correspondence created a parallel record-accuracy and restriction process. Those documents establish requests, responses and institutional positions. They do not themselves decide whether the underlying clinical account was medically wrong or legally inaccurate.

Why provenance matters here

Later institutional repetition should not be treated as independent corroboration merely because the same historical proposition appears in more than one governance document. A 2025 complaint response, PAR, SIMT outcome and regulatory submission may all descend from the same earlier clinical record or later clinician explanation.

The audit layer therefore keeps separate the source that first establishes a proposition from later documents that repeat, adopt or review it.

What remains to be obtained

The highest-value governance disclosure remains the complete PAR and source schedule, followed by the SIMT agenda, minutes and circulation pack. Those materials would allow counsel to distinguish a genuinely independent re-examination from a process that relied mainly on the inherited clinical narrative.

Evidence boundary. The available record proves a formal governance process and a no-deficit outcome. It does not yet permit the repository to reconstruct the review’s proposition-level evidential basis.
