Galway University Hospitals  
Quality & Patient Safety Department GUH

Private & Confidential

Dear Mr Liam Moloney,

I refer to your complaint received by the Quality and Patient Safety Department on the 25th of March in relation to concerns of bias and diagnostic anchoring, that you feel affected how your condition was managed, in particular in relation to pain management.

You recently started Sinemet CR, which has been an effective analgesic for your foot pain. However, you feel this particular medication was not considered by your Neurologist, possibly due to an emphasis on your history of alcohol excess, which led to prolonged and preventable pain.

When a complaint is received by the Complaints Officer and, following its assessment, is determined to relate to matters solely relating to clinical judgment, it must be sent to the appropriate manager for follow-up under the appropriate pathway in accordance with the Health Act 2004. In this regard, your complaint was forwarded for the attention of Consultant Neurologist Dr Thomas Monaghan. I include overleaf a letter from Dr Monaghan in response to the complaint.

In relation to your request for your medical notes, you can request these through Freedom of Information (FOI). I have also enclosed information on how to do this.

Thank you for bringing your concerns to our attention and for the opportunity to respond. We endeavour to improve our service at every opportunity to ensure we provide a high-quality service to those in our care.

With kind regards,

Aoife Nolan  
Quality & Patient Safety Coordinator and Complaints Officer  
Quality and Patient Safety Department  
Galway University Hospital
