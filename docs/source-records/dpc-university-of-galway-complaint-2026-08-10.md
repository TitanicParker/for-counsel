## Complaint submitted

On 10 August 2026, Liam Moloney submitted a separate GDPR complaint to the Data Protection Commission concerning **University of Galway**, following the DPC's clarification that University of Galway is a separate controller from HSE / Galway University Hospitals for the relevant issues.

The complaint was framed around Articles 15, 16 and 18 and relied on the earlier University-directed sequence beginning with the 24 November 2025 request concerning University-linked teaching data arising from the reported July 2017 bedside teaching interaction.

The complaint materials included the contemporaneous University correspondence and a procedural-provenance document.

Later on 10 August 2026, the Data Protection Commission acknowledged the submission and assigned:

> **DPC case reference `DPC0826812075`**

## Earlier University rights sequence preserved in the complaint materials

The source bundle includes the following earlier University-directed chronology:

- **24 November 2025** — written request engaging Articles 15, 16 and 18 concerning any University-held personal data arising from the 2017 bedside teaching interaction, including trainee records, teaching notes, supervisory material and academic-governance records;
- **8 December 2025** — reminder noting no acknowledgement or clarification after 14 days;
- **24 December 2025** — notice recording expiry of the one-month period, no substantive response, no extension notice, no confirmation of Article 18 restriction and no position on whether relevant University-held data existed;
- **31 December 2025** — Article 77 complaint sent to the DPC concerning University of Galway, then framed for inclusion on the existing file for procedural efficiency.

The DPC later clarified in August 2026 that University of Galway should be treated as a separate controller for complaint purposes, leading to the fresh 10 August 2026 complaint and separate case reference.

## Source-status boundary
