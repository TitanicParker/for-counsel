**Source type:** primary institutional correspondence

Dear Mr. Moloney,

I have received communication arising from the Office of the Ombudsman in relation to apparent concerns which you have raised about the management of your complaint, which was first received by the Quality and Patient Safety Department on the 25th of March 2025.

I understand you have expressed concern with regard to the objectivity of the hospital’s approach to the management of your complaint. The purpose of my correspondence to you today is to provide you with additional clarity with regard to how complaints are managed.

The HSE Your Service Your Say (YSYS) Policy 2017 is underpinned by Part 9 of the 2004 Health Act and 2006 Complaints Regulations. This legislation exempts certain matters for examination under the YSYS policy. Section 48(1)(a) outlines that a person is not entitled to make a complaint about a matter relating solely to the exercise of clinical judgement by a person acting on behalf of the Executive (HSE).

As outlined in the letter of response issued on the 15th of April, a complaint that relates to matters of clinical judgment is always made available to the clinician whom the complaint is about, for their observations and response where appropriate. This is to afford the clinician an opportunity to respond to what has been alleged. Please note therefore that the role of a Complaints Officer in the case of matters of clinical judgment is not to investigate the matter, but to simply coordinate the management of the complaint. This involves ensuring that the complaint is received by the correct clinician, and that a response is provided back to the complainant. It is important to emphasise here that as matters of clinical judgement are exempt from examination under the YSYS policy, they are not subject to a Stage 3 and Stage 4 Review.

As you were not reassured by the response provided to you on 25th April, I consulted with the Clinical Director for Medicine in GUH, who agreed to have your complaint reviewed independently by the hospital’s incident management team to allay your concerns about transparency. You were advised of this in a letter issued to you on the 30th of May.

### YSYS

My role is to ensure a complaint that relates to a clinical issue is referred to the respective clinician who provided care, and to issue this response to the complainant in due course.

### Incident Management Framework (IMF)

Your complaint will be reviewed independently by an incident management team in accordance with this framework.

### Ongoing correspondence

I wish to thank you for your correspondence to date and the information which you have provided. Given the volume of correspondence received to date in relation to your complaint, and my commitment to a high volume of current open cases within my remit, I will require some time to review, collate and coordinate your complaint. In that regard, I ask that you kindly refrain from forwarding me any further correspondence and documentation at this time as this will only deter from advancing and progressing your complaint. I undertake to revert to you once the relevant personnel have reviewed your case and a response has been prepared.

I hope my correspondence has provided you with more clarity on the process and assurance that I play no part in the examination and further review of your complaint.

Yours sincerely,

Aoife Nolan  
Quality & Patient Safety Coordinator and Complaints Officer  
Quality and Patient Safety Department  
Galway University Hospital
