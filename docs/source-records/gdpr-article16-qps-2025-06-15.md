# 15 June 2025 — Article 16 request concerning QPS closure-letter material

**Source type:** primary data-subject correspondence  
**Author:** Liam Moloney  
**Recipients:** GUH DPO; Dean of Medical Education  
**CC:** QPS GUH  
**Date:** 15 June 2025  
**Subject:** `GDPR Art. 16 & Health Act 2004 Rectification Request — QPS “Closure Letter” Template`  
**Provenance:** transcribed directly from the connected Gmail account / preserved primary correspondence text.  
**Status:** faithful redacted transcription. The original Gmail message remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

The request invoked Article 16 GDPR and sought rectification of QPS closure-letter material which, in the author's view, invoked the Health Act 2004 and used “assessment” to classify complaints as solely clinical without independent review or appeal.

The correspondence asked that materially similar documents be identified and annotated or withdrawn in secondary/educational contexts, requested an audit trail of any correction/withdrawal, and attached `Exhibit A.jpg`, identified in the email as the 15 April 2025 Nolan QPS closure letter.

The request expressly characterised the 15 April letter and similar material as inaccurate and sought annotation under Article 16.
