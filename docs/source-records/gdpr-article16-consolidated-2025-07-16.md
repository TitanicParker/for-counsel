# 16 July 2025 — Consolidated Article 16 rectification correspondence

**Source type:** primary data-subject correspondence  
**Date:** email sent 16 July 2025; attached document internally dated 15 July 2025  
**Subject:** `Final GDPR Article 16 Rectification Request – Patient ID 0360424`  
**Provenance:** complete substantive email and attachment text supplied to the project.  
**Status:** redacted source transcription / source summary. Original email and attached PDF remain controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

The email described itself as a final and comprehensive Article 16 request following the 12 June and 15 June requests and the 7 July reminders. It alleged non-compliance with Article 12(3), invited corrective action, and attached a document titled **“Final Article 16 GDPR Rectification Request — Rectification of Structurally Contradictory Medical Record.”**

The attachment advanced the data subject's then-current case that the medical record was structurally inaccurate or incomplete. Among other things, it relied on the absence of a diagnostic levodopa trial, the bilateral foot-pain history, the 2025 dopamine-response discovery, and concerns about how specialist expertise and later complaint handling were represented. It sought rectification/annotation of diagnostic labels and summaries, restrictions on secondary use, and disclosure of governance decisions and teaching use.

The document also contained strong legal and clinical assertions, including that the record was “structurally and clinically self-nullifying,” that the 2025 response “disproved” earlier assumptions, and that the diagnosis was unsound.
