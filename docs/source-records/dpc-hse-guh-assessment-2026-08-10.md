---

The Data Protection Commission referred to correspondence received on 8 August 2026 and stated that the issues and information provided concerning the complaint against **HSE (University Hospital Galway)** were:

> **“under assessment by this office”**

and that a further update would issue.

The DPC invited any additional information the data subject wished to provide before 14 August 2026 and directed that it be sent to the Complaint Assessment & Early Resolution address quoting the existing case reference.

The DPC separately clarified that concerns involving **University of Galway** could be submitted as a separate complaint because that institution is a separate controller for the purposes of the matter being discussed.

---

## Immediate preceding regulatory request — 7 August 2026

In correspondence of 7 August 2026, the DPC identified three data-rights strands requiring further material in relation to HSE / Galway University Hospitals:

1. **Article 16** — multiple rectification requests and the controller's grounds for refusal;
2. **Article 15** — the targeted access request and what personal data remained outstanding;
3. **Article 18** — the method and date by which restriction of processing had been invoked.

The DPC also cautioned that medical material consisting of professional opinion may not be rectifiable merely because the data subject disagrees with it. That was a preliminary regulatory observation, not a final determination of the complaint.

---

## Supplementary primary-evidence submission — 10 August 2026

Later on 10 August 2026, the data subject supplied the DPC with separately dated primary correspondence and a manifest. The submission expressly narrowed the questions to the administration of Articles 15, 16 and 18 rather than asking the DPC to decide clinical correctness.

Among the questions placed before the Commission were:

- whether the two separate June 2025 Article 16 requests were individually identified and determined;
- whether any Article 12(3) extension was communicated;
- whether the 12 June request for **rectification and annotation** was correctly determined when the 6 September decision introduced it as a request for **erasure**;
- what procedural treatment was given to the express 4 August 2025 Article 18 restriction request before the SIMT process;
- what the 5 August 2025 Article 15 response meant by both “refuse you access to some of these records” and later non-holding statements;
- and whether the renewed Article 16 / Article 18 exercise of 14 January 2026 received a separate data-protection determination.

This source establishes the **present procedural status** that, on 10 August 2026, the HSE / Galway University Hospitals complaint was under DPC assessment.

It does **not** establish that any GDPR infringement occurred, that the DPC has accepted the data subject's legal characterisation, or that any clinical proposition has been determined by the regulator.
