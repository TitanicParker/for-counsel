Dear Mr Moloney,

I am writing to you in respect of the complaint you made in relation to Dr Timothy Counihan received on 9 May 2025. Under the Medical Practitioners Act 2007, as amended (“the Act”), complaints are made to, and investigated by, the Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”) of the Medical Council.

Please note that the CEO has made a decision not to investigate your complaint against Dr Counihan. The CEO’s decision and the basis for it are explained further below.

## The Decision of the CEO

Before a decision is made to investigate a complaint, the CEO is required to consider whether he or she:

i. is satisfied that the complaint is not made in good faith, or  
ii. is satisfied that the complaint is frivolous or vexatious.

If the CEO decides that a complaint comes within one of the above categories, the complaint will be closed. If the complaint does not come within one of these categories, then the CEO is required to investigate the complaint.

The CEO has considered this complaint and decided that the complaint was frivolous and/or vexatious in circumstances where Dr Counihan was **not directly involved in your care** and was satisfied that the complaint did not raise any clinical concerns or patient safety issues in relation to Dr Counihan and could never reach the threshold of seriousness to warrant a referral to the Fitness to Practise Committee.

## What Happens Next

Please note that the CEO has made a decision not to investigate your complaint against Dr Counihan. On that basis, no further action will be taken in relation to your complaint against Dr Counihan.

An Authorised Officer will be in touch with you in due course in relation to your complaint against Dr Thomas Monaghan.

Please note that a copy of your complaint together with the CEO’s decision outlined above has also been sent to Dr Counihan for his information.

## General Information regarding the Medical Council Complaint Process

The Medical Council is the regulatory body for doctors. It has a statutory role in protecting the public by promoting the highest professional standards amongst doctors practising in the Republic of Ireland. One of the functions of the Medical Council is to consider complaints against registered medical practitioners. The CEO of the Medical Council considers and investigates complaints against registered medical practitioners. Part 7 of the Act is the part that relates to the consideration of complaints by the CEO. Section 57 (2B) is the section of the Act that deals with the triaging of complaints by the CEO.

Should you have any further queries please do not hesitate to contact complaints@mcirl.ie.
