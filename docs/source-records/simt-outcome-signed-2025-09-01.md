Dear Mr. Moloney,

I am writing to follow up on the Preliminary Assessment Report (PAR) conducted regarding the care you received from consultant Neurologist, Dr. Thomas Monaghan between July 2017 and March 2025. The PAR was initiated as part of our review process in response to your complaint that outlined concerns of bias and diagnostic anchoring that you feel affected your care. As a result, you allege Sinemet CR was not prescribed until March 2025, resulting in prolonged foot pain.

The PAR was presented to the Serious Incident Management Team (SIMT) on 27/8/2025. This meeting was chaired by Dr. Ramona McLoughlin, Clinical Director for Quality & Patient Safety HSE West and North West. Following presentation of the PAR, the multidisciplinary membership of the SIMT were satisfied that decisions made in relation to care at Galway University Hospital were appropriate. The SIMT were reassured by the clinical notes that evidence concern and careful consideration about starting Sinemet at such an early age, given the risk of associated side-effects. Alternative potential diagnoses were considered and Sinemet was first prescribed in 2021, therefore it was determined that there was no deficit in the care provided.

Yours sincerely,

Ms. Aoife Nolan  
Quality and Patient Safety Coordinator, Galway University Hospital

Professor Tim O'Brien  
Clinical Director, Medicine MCAN  
HSE West and North West
