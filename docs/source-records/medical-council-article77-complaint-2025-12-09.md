# 9 December 2025 — Article 77 GDPR complaint concerning the Medical Council of Ireland

**Source type:** primary data-subject regulatory complaint  
**Controller complained of:** Medical Council of Ireland  
**Date submitted:** 9 December 2025  
**Provenance:** complaint email and attached eight-page PDF read directly from the connected Gmail account.  
**Status:** redacted source summary. The original email and PDF remain controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

Liam Moloney submitted a formal complaint to the Data Protection Commission under Article 77 GDPR concerning the Medical Council's refusal to rectify, erase, restrict or notify concerning personal data arising from its 29 July 2025 decision.

The complaint alleged that the decision recorded that Dr Counihan was not directly involved in the data subject's care and that the correspondence was treated as frivolous or vexatious, while the Council also held documentary material on 29 July 2025 which the complainant said showed Counihan involvement.

The complaint recorded that:

- the alleged inaccuracy was notified to the Council on 8 September 2025;
- a formal request under Articles 16, 17, 18 and 19 was submitted on 18 November 2025;
- correspondence continued between 20 November and 8 December 2025;
- and the Council ultimately refused the requested rights on the basis, as characterised by the complainant, that the July decision remained a historical record of what the CEO considered at the time.

The complaint asked the DPC to determine, among other things:

- the application of Article 5(1)(d) accuracy to the disputed factual proposition;
- whether the decision could be treated as a fixed historical record immune from correction despite allegedly contradictory material;
- the Council's interpretation of Article 18;
- and whether continued processing of the disputed data was lawful.
