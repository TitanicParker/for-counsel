Dear Liam,

I refer to our previous letter dated 30th of May, in which we advised you that your complaint is being investigated through the hospital's Incident Management Framework (IMF).

While this investigation remains ongoing and you have the right to communicate, I am now formally asking that you refrain from any further correspondence with the QPS department while this review is ongoing, unless you have new information or a new concern to raise.

You have provided considerable correspondence since the 30th of May letter and, as previously stated, all your correspondence will be taken into consideration as part of the review.

We want to ensure that the review is conducted in a careful and appropriate manner, and the volume of correspondence is impacting on the effectiveness of the process.

I appreciate your cooperation, and we will contact you with the outcome of the review once completed.

Yours sincerely,

Aoife Nolan  
Quality & Patient Safety Coordinator and Complaints Officer

Galway University Hospital
