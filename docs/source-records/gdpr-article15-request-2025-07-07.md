# 7 July 2025 — Article 15 access request: teaching interaction

**Source type:** primary data-subject correspondence  
**Date:** 7 July 2025  
**Subject:** `Article 15 GDPR Access Request – Teaching Interaction (St. Anthony’s Ward, 10–11 July 2017)`  
**Provenance:** complete substantive text supplied to the project and previously verified against the connected Gmail account.  
**Status:** redacted source transcription. The original message remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

The data subject requested access under Article 15 GDPR to personal data and institutional documentation concerning a bedside teaching interaction during the July 2017 admission.

The request recorded the data subject's account that four medical trainees were present; that he raised concern that disabling bilateral foot pain had not been addressed; that he asked for the supervising neurologist; and that a discussion followed concerning podiatry.

The request sought:

- clinical documentation from 10–11 July 2017 referring to the interaction or student involvement;
- student logbooks, observation records or teaching notes;
- ward-round records, consultant entries or trainee annotations referring to symptoms, foot pain or the teaching interaction;
- governance, supervisory or educational material describing bedside teaching on the ward during the period; and
- trainee assignment records or rosters identifying students present.

The request asked that third-party information be redacted rather than used as a basis for wholesale refusal.
