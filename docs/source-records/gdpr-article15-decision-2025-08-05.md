# 5 August 2025 — Article 15 access decision

**Source type:** primary administrative / data-rights decision  
**Author/signatory:** Nuala Madden, SARS Co-ordinator, Galway University Hospitals  
**Recipient:** Liam Moloney  
**Date:** 5 August 2025  
**Underlying request:** 7 July 2025 Article 15 request concerning bedside teaching interaction / student involvement / ward-round and related governance records  
**Provenance:** transcribed from the primary decision preserved in the connected Gmail archive.  
**Status:** faithful redacted transcription of the substantive decision. The original letter remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

The decision states that Galway University Hospitals did not hold personal data responsive to several requested categories, including student involvement, student records, 2017 ward-round records, governance/supervisory/education documents describing teaching on St Anthony’s Ward, and trainee assignment records.

It separately states that the Podiatry Department in Unit 3, Merlin Park University Hospital is part of Community Healthcare West rather than a hospital service, and directs requests for podiatry records to the relevant community-services SARS route.

The decision also says that relevant doctors’ clinical notes, nursing notes and radiology reports for the requested period were enclosed.

---

**Source-boundary note:** This decision establishes the controller’s stated search/non-holding position and the administrative routing of podiatry records. It does not establish that the reported bedside teaching/protest event did not occur, and it does not by itself show the search methodology or completeness of the institutional search.
