# 12 September 2017 — Prof Daniel G. Healy response after specialist review

**Source type:** primary contemporaneous specialist correspondence  
**Author:** Prof Daniel G. Healy, Consultant Neurologist  
**Recipient:** Dr Thomas Monaghan, Consultant Neurologist, Galway University Hospital  
**Clinic date:** 12 September 2017  
**Provenance:** photographed signed/typed letter supplied directly by Liam Moloney to the project on 12 August 2026 and visually inspected.  
**Status:** redacted partial transcription of the clearly legible substantive passages. The photographed original remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

Dear Tom,

> “Thank you for asking me to see this man who I think probably has Park II and I would suggest testing that initially. If it is negative I would go on to test a panel.”

> “It is a little bit unclear but he probably developed Parkinsonism somewhere between his late 20s and early 30s.”

The letter then discusses background/family-history considerations and possible heterozygous manifestations. Some words in that paragraph are not reproduced here because the supplied image is not sufficiently clear to guarantee a literal transcription of every word.

> “There are very few non motor symptoms. He was able to detect 7/10 smells on the UPSIT scale. He describes a dramatic improvement in his motor symptoms with Procyclidine low dose qds and Rasagiline 1mg od. He takes Clonazepam at night.”

Prof Healy records review of the MRI brain and advises keeping an open mind about a subtle imaging point if PARK II testing is negative. The exact wording of that imaging sentence should be taken from the original image rather than this partial transcript.

> “He had a lot of questions which I answered to the best of my ability.”

> “Physical activity would be helpful and as you are aware patients with PARK II tend to manage quite well for long periods of time with relatively low doses of Dopamine replacement therapy. They can become quite dyskinetic if over-used. One should mentally think of it as a nigropathy.”

> “I have not arranged to see him again. I have given him his CDs back. I would be very interested in the results of the tests and happy to see in the future as required.”

Yours sincerely,

Prof. Daniel G. Healy  
Consultant Neurologist

## Source-status note

This letter directly establishes what Prof Healy himself recorded after seeing Liam in September 2017. In particular it directly records:

- Healy's opinion that Liam **“probably has Park II”**;
- his recommendation to test that initially and move to a panel if negative;
- his estimate that Parkinsonism probably developed between the late 20s and early 30s;
- the reported dramatic motor improvement with Procyclidine and Rasagiline;
- his statement that PARK II patients can manage for long periods on relatively low doses of dopamine replacement therapy while becoming dyskinetic if over-used;
- his interest in the test results and willingness to review again if required.

This source does **not** state that Prof Healy ordered immediate commencement of levodopa. It also does not by itself establish what Galway subsequently did with the advice. Those are separate analytical questions.
