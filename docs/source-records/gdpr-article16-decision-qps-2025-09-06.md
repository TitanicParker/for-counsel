# 6 September 2025 - Article 16 decision concerning QPS closure letters

**Source type:** primary administrative / data-rights decision  
**Author/signatory:** Nuala Madden, SARS Coordinator, Galway University Hospitals

**Date:** 6 September 2025  
**Underlying request:** Article 16 request dated 15 June 2025 concerning QPS closure letters

**Status:** faithful redacted transcription. The original PDF remains the controlling source. No analytical findings are made on this page.

Dear Mr. Moloney,

I refer to your request dated 15th June, 2025 made under Article 16 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for the:

> “Immediate rectification of all Quality and Patient Safety (QPS) ‘closure letters’ issued by Galway University Hospital that
>
> - Invoke the Health Act 2004 as justification for rerouting complaints back to the original clinician; and
> - Use the term ‘assessment’ to describe an undocumented non-transparent internal process that classifies complaints as ‘solely clinical’ without independent review or appeal.”

I wish to advise that the GDPR does provide individuals with the right to rectification of inaccurate personal data and the right to erasure in specific circumstances. However, your request does not seem to relate to the rectification of your personal data.

Personal data as defined under the GDPR means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (‘data subject’); therefore a Quality and Patient Safety “closure letters” issued by Galway University Hospital does not relate to you as a data subject.

Therefore, my decision is to refuse this request for the amendment of records, as it does not relate to your personal data and does not come within the scope of personal data as defined under the GDPR.

If you are unhappy with this decision, you may make a complaint in writing to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Yours sincerely,

Nuala Madden  
SARS Coordinator  
Galway University Hospitals
