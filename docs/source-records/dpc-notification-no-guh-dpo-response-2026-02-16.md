# 16 February 2026 — DPC notification concerning renewed Article 16 / 18 engagement

**Source type:** primary data-subject correspondence to regulator  
**Date:** 16 February 2026  
**Subject:** `C-25-7-2074 — Article 12(3) Expiry Notification (Renewed Article 16 / 18 Engagement)`  
**Provenance:** complete substantive text supplied to the project.  
**Status:** redacted source transcription. Original Gmail message remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

The data subject notified the Data Protection Commission that the renewed Article 16 / Article 18 engagement of 14 January 2026 had, in his view, passed the one-month Article 12(3) response period.

The message stated that as of 16 February 2026:

- no acknowledgment had been received from the controller;
- no extension notice had been issued; and
- no substantive response had been provided.

It asked the Commission to determine whether the controller was compliant with Article 12(3) and with its handling obligations under Articles 16 and 18.

---

**Source-boundary note:** This source proves that the non-response position was reported to the DPC on 16 February 2026. Whether a legal deadline had expired, whether Article 18 remained in force, and whether any breach occurred are legal questions not determined by this correspondence alone.
