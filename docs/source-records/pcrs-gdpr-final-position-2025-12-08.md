## 8 December 2025 — substantive PCRS position

The PCRS letter addressed the disputed date associated with Liam Moloney's Long Term Illness (LTI) application.

PCRS stated that LTI submissions can be made by a consultant in advance of final diagnosis where a clinical diagnosis is strongly indicated, describing that as standard procedure intended to expedite access to treatment.

PCRS then stated, in substance, that under Article 5(1)(d) GDPR the date held by PCRS was accurate **“in the context of the LTI scheme”** because it reflected the certified application form submitted on 9 July 2017, and that the existing record would remain.

PCRS also stated that the clinically confirmed diagnosis date of 13 July 2017 had been added to the record for transparency, and said that this did not attribute chronology or endorsement to the data subject.
