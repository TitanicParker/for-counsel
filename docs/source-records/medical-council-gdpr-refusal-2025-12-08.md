# 8 December 2025 — Medical Council GDPR refusal position

**Source type:** primary controller correspondence  
**Controller:** Medical Council of Ireland  
**Author:** Head of Information Governance  
**Date:** 8 December 2025  
**Provenance:** read directly from the connected Gmail correspondence thread.  
**Status:** redacted source summary. The original email remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

The Medical Council responded to a request concerning personal data arising from its 29 July 2025 decision.

The Council understood the disputed data to include the decision wording that Dr Counihan was not directly involved in Liam Moloney's care and the disposition of the earlier correspondence.

The Council refused rectification under Article 16. Its stated reasoning was that the CEO's decision was based on the information available to and before the CEO at the time of review, and that information received afterwards did not alter that historical decision.

The Council also refused erasure under Article 17, referring to its records-management and retention obligations, and refused restriction under Article 18 on substantially the same historical-decision reasoning. It stated that Article 19 did not apply because there had been no rectification, erasure or restriction.

The surrounding correspondence shows that the data subject disputed the factual premise of non-involvement and contended that material held by the Council on 29 July 2025 itself supported involvement. He sought to distinguish correction of personal data from reopening a professional-conduct complaint.

---

## Article 18 exchange

In the preceding correspondence, the Council stated that it needed to review the complaint file to investigate the data-rights request and took the position that restriction would prevent that review without the data subject's consent.

The data subject disputed that interpretation and sought clarification of the purpose and scope of processing.

This page records the competing positions. It does not determine the correct interpretation of Article 18(2).

---

## Source-status boundary

This source establishes the controller's communicated GDPR position on 8 December 2025.

It does not establish whether the Council's factual premise was correct, whether contradictory evidence was legally material to accuracy, or whether any refusal breached the GDPR.
