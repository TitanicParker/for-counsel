# 30 May 2025 — QPS letter: Incident Management Framework / PAR / SIMT

**Source type:** primary institutional correspondence  
**Author/signatory:** Aoife Nolan, Quality & Patient Safety Coordinator and Complaints Officer, Galway University Hospital  
**Recipient:** Liam Moloney  
**Date:** 30 May 2025  
**Provenance:** transcribed directly from a photographed original letter preserved in Gmail and visually inspected in the project.  
**Status:** faithful redacted transcription of the substantive correspondence. The source image remains controlling. No analytical findings are made on this page.

Dear Mr. Liam Moloney,

I refer to your complaint first received by the Quality and Patient Safety Department on the 25th of March in relation to concerns of bias and diagnostic anchoring that you feel affected your care management at GUH.

A letter of response was issued on the 15th of April, however, as you remain dissatisfied a decision has been made to manage your complaint through the hospital’s Incident Management Framework (IMF).

As part of this a Preliminary Assessment Report (PAR) is being completed and this will be reviewed at the IHA West and North West Serious Incident Management Team (SIMT) meeting in the coming weeks.

Following the SIMT meeting, I will inform you of the findings. The Quality and Patient Safety Department are available to speak to you by phone or in person if you have any questions or concerns at any point during this process. If you want clarity on how this process is managed, we are happy to explain the details.

In the meantime, if you have any questions you can email QPS or contact the department by telephone.

Yours sincerely,

Aoife Nolan  
Quality & Patient Safety Coordinator and Complaints Officer  
Quality and Patient Safety Department  
Galway University Hospital
