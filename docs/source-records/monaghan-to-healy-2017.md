# 2017 — Monaghan correspondence to Prof Dan Healy

**Source type:** primary contemporaneous clinician-to-clinician correspondence  
**Author:** Dr Thomas Monaghan  
**Recipient:** Prof Dan Healy, Consultant Neurologist, Beaumont Hospital, Dublin 9  
**Date:** 2017; exact date not visible on the preserved image supplied through Gmail  
**Provenance:** transcribed directly from a photographed/scanned image preserved in Gmail and visually verified in the project.  
**Status:** faithful redacted transcription of the substantive clinical correspondence. The original image remains the controlling source. No analytical findings are made on this page.

---

Dear Dan,

I wonder if I might seek your advice please as regards Mr Moloney.

Liam is remarkable young man who progressed very far on Channel 4s Countdown in recent years.

Unfortunately Liam seems to have had symptoms of Parkinson's for the last 13 years. He is now age 39 at current presentation, and this is his first time being seen by Neurology.

I would be grateful for advice on what genetic testing might be appropriate in the circumstances and more particularly, whether you thing this is reasonable and likely to help Liam?

In particular **would you have genetic contacts who might analyse DNA? UHG will pay wherever the NCMG send samples of course. I have looked at Centogenes proposals as enclosed.**

I have his imaging enclosed on disc.

If you felt it might be of benefit to him to be seen in Dublin, Liam would be happy to be seen publically or privately for a once off opinion.

**Myself and Tim are keen to hold back on Levodopa insofar as maybe possible unless you'd advise otherwise?**

We have made a Video which Liam had consented to for Clinical Purposes only --- and this includes the condition of provision to Beaumont for a second opinion.

With thanks and kind regards,

Thomas

## Source-boundary note

The image itself identifies the author as Thomas and the recipient as Prof Dan Healy. It **does not identify who “Tim” is**. Any identification of “Tim” as Prof Timothy Counihan comes from separate later sources and must not be back-written into this 2017 document.

The letter directly establishes a contemporaneous second-opinion/genetics request and a shared levodopa-deferral position involving the author and a person called Tim. It does not by itself establish the authority, attendance, examination role or final decision-making status of any other clinician.
