## GUH acknowledgment

**Sender:** Nuala Madden, SARS Co-ordinator, Galway University Hospitals  
**Subject:** `Acknowledgement of your GDPR Request.`

GUH acknowledged receipt of a rectification request, stated that rectification is a GDPR right but not an absolute right, said the request required careful review and consideration, and described it as a work in progress.

## Same-day clarification response

The data subject replied seeking clarification as to which of three requests the acknowledgment referred to: the 12 June Article 16 clinical-record request, the 15 June Article 16 QPS request, or the 7 July Article 15 access request.

The reply asked GUH to confirm whether the requests had been separately logged and distinguished, whether any Article 12(3) extension had issued concerning the 12 June request, who was responsible for processing each request, and whether the clinical record would be corrected, annotated or left unchanged and on what basis.

It also repeated the author's legal position concerning the applicable deadlines and transparency/accountability obligations.
