Repetition in the Patient Statement Log or later analysis is not independent corroboration. Liam’s account is also explicit that he did not mention levodopa during the protest.

That does not determine clinical appropriateness, negligence, conscious omission, reckless reliance, knowing reconstruction, dishonesty, fraud or deliberate concealment.
