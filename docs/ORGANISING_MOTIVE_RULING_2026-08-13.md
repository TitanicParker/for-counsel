# Organising Motive Ruling — Protection of the July 2017 Levodopa-Deferral Decision

**Date:** 13 August 2026

**Purpose:** incorporate the findings of the organising-motive audit into the contracted case without expanding it into a general nine-year documentation study.

## Core ruling

The strongest present comparative state-of-mind conclusion is:

> **Protection of the July 2017 senior-backed levodopa-deferral architecture is a strong organising-motive hypothesis and the best overall explanation of the directional documentary pattern.**

This is not a finding that Monaghan, Counihan or any institutional actor deliberately set out in 2017 to conceal error or deceive the patient. The distinction is:

- **architecture-preserving effect:** strongly demonstrated;
- **subjective protective motive:** strongly arguable and, by 2025, the best overall motive hypothesis;
- **knowing/dishonest motive:** not proved more likely than not;
- **deliberate concealment, conspiracy or legal fraud:** premature.

The most realistic composite account remains:

> **initial sincere caution and path dependence, followed by conscious accommodation and potentially reckless defensive reconstruction once the July architecture came under express challenge.**

## What was being protected

The protected object was not merely a single decision not to start levodopa on one day.

It was the legitimacy of an architecture under which:

- ordinary levodopa and dopamine agonists were to be avoided for as long as possible;
- other symptomatic measures were used first;
- young-age dyskinesia and treatment-complication risk dominated the immediate balance;
- possible substantial benefit and possible later clinician regret were acknowledged but subordinated to deferral;
- genetics was treated as generally unlikely to change management;
- the feet remained `Not clear` while Podiatry became operative;
- senior/colleague support was invoked;
- the patient was recorded as having `good understanding` of the rationale, qualified `at least for now`;
- no documented global reopening rule specified what later evidence would require reconsideration of the architecture itself.

## Why the motive hypothesis now matters

The later record repeatedly shows the same directional effect:

- evidence supportive of the original architecture receives broad historical or justificatory weight;
- evidence threatening the architecture is accepted locally, reclassified, narrowed, treated as mixed/nonspecific, or denied equivalent retrospective force;
- authority expands where useful to legitimate the original policy and contracts when responsibility is later allocated;
- specialist review is retained as proof of scrutiny while its destabilising content is not visibly adjudicated;
- clinician-owned prospective regret becomes patient retrospective preference;
- unresolved genetic suspicion later hardens toward idiopathic certainty without an identified bridge;
- later treatment rationale is projected backward over pregabalin's actual origin;
- dopaminergic foot relief changes treatment but is contained historically by `of whatever cause` pain modulation;
- later formal dystonia is treated as `subsequent` without demonstrated lineage review.

The challenged transformations are not uniformly protective. The record also contains substantial disclosure, apology, admissions, acknowledgement of Sinemet benefit and `profound relief`, possible error, and an express statement that another diagnosis would be `embarrassing`. Those facts materially oppose a theory of a fully sanitised deception.

The significance is therefore directionality, not purity.

## Chronological threshold

### July 2017

**Conscious policy formation and awareness of risk.**

No improper motive is established.

### September 2017

**External vulnerability.** Healy's probable PARK II assessment and relatively low dopamine-replacement / avoid-over-use formulation created a genuine opportunity for the policy to be reconsidered.

### April 2018

**Conscious policy maintenance.** Monaghan personally recorded medication-sensitive foot evidence, suspected PARK2/PRKN, potential major levodopa benefit and continued prolonged deferral.

### 2020

**Increased awareness of threat.** The foot throw was recorded as `actually parkinsonian` with normal power.

### 2023

**Knowledge that the architecture was threatened.** OFF dystonia, undertreatment, Stalevo benefit, earliest symptoms in the feet and express clinician–patient `misalignment` were all within the same continuity record.

### March–July 2025

**Conscious interpretive accommodation and reasonable basis to investigate reckless reconstruction.** The historical account was actively assembled and deployed under complaint/regulatory conditions while the clinician possessed the earlier sources and fresh dopaminergic foot-response evidence.

## The four strongest motive indicators

1. **Regret redistribution**
   - 2017: `we may regret that we did not start this earlier`;
   - later: patient may wish it had been started earlier.

   The anticipated consequence moves from clinician judgment to patient hindsight/preference.

2. **Counihan authority contraction**
   - formation: senior/colleague caution and `Myself and Tim`;
   - accountability: advice `only what I already knew`, `not involved`, later narrowed to `not directly involved`.

3. **Healy selective invocation**
   - Healy genuinely reviewed the patient and did not order immediate levodopa;
   - he nevertheless supplied a materially distinguishable cautious low-dose replacement model;
   - the record later invokes `a second opinion` while no identified source adjudicates that distinction against maximum postponement.

4. **Pregabalin retrospective purpose**
   - source chronology: GP initiation for severe pain and escalation through various doctors;
   - later account: `introduced ... in part to try to spare further Sinemet increase`.

   This is presently the cleanest discrete candidate for reckless or potentially knowing reconstruction because source access was strong and the fact-purpose mismatch is comparatively simple.

## Relationship to `good understanding`

The organising-motive audit confirms the separate reliability ruling:

> **`Good understanding` is foundational protective documentation only if it is permitted to prove more than its literal content.**

It proves, at highest, a clinician's recorded assessment that the patient understood the selected rationale for immediate delay, `at least for now`.

It does not prove informed acceptance of the stronger policy `for as long as possible`, agreement, consent, absence of dissent, full disclosure of ownership/revisability, or consensual closure.

The mismatch between the patient-time qualifier and the management-time objective is materially important.

## Relationship to the protest

### Scenario A — no independent corroboration

The reported bedside protest remains patient evidence. It does not establish omission as historical fact. `Good understanding` still cannot prove agreement or absence of dissent.

### Scenario B — independent corroboration

If one or more independent students credibly corroborate the essential protest, the motive case strengthens substantially because a direct challenge to the architecture would have been known before finalisation while the durable document retained `Not clear`, Podiatry, rationale and `good understanding` without the challenge.

That would create a materially misleading participation/closure narrative unless the record expressly preserved comprehension alongside disagreement.

It would still not, by itself, prove deliberate concealment.

## Relationship to record impeachment

This ruling does not justify rejecting the entire chart.

The record retains ordinary weight for objective observations, tests, prescriptions and proof of what clinicians recorded or decided.

Presumptive narrative reliability should remain withdrawn or reduced for disputed clinician-controlled propositions concerning:

- patient position and agreement;
- completeness of disclosure;
- absence of dissent;
- decision ownership and authority;
- causal allocation;
- specialist validation;
- historical legitimacy;
- later governance validation where source independence is not demonstrated.

The hospital may prove that a clinician **said** or **recorded** X. It may not use that representation by itself to prove the truth, completeness or independent legitimacy of X where the originating narrative is materially impeached.

## Contracted case impact

The overall case remains:

> **Decision → contradiction → preservation → reconstruction.**

This motive ruling primarily strengthens **Preservation** and **Reconstruction**.

The two central argumentative questions remain:

> **Why did evidence capable of changing the July 2017 decision not visibly change the July 2017 decision?**

and

> **When the July decision later required defence, why did the retrospective account repeatedly differ from the contemporaneous sources in ways that improved legitimacy, reduced responsibility, or deprived contrary evidence of historical force?**

## Strongest falsifier

The motive hypothesis would be materially weakened by a contemporaneous, independently authenticated record showing:

- full disclosure of maximum postponement and decision ownership;
- defined reopening criteria;
- patient understanding/teach-back and actual position;
- explicit reconciliation of Healy's low-dose replacement model with the Galway policy;
- documented global reconsideration when later criteria were met;
- reasoned reassessment of the original foot/Podiatry partition;
- candid later acknowledgement that the original policy may have delayed worthwhile symptomatic benefit.

## Current state-of-mind ruling

The strongest fair conclusion is:

> **Protection of the July 2017 decision predicts the later documentary behaviour better than any single innocent alternative and, with moderate confidence, better than the full benign conjunction. It explains why acknowledged facts repeatedly changed treatment without acquiring equivalent historical, causal, participation or responsibility consequences.**

But:

> **The evidence does not safely establish that protection was a consciously dishonest organising motive in each actor's mind.**

Accordingly, the proper classification remains:

- strong organising-motive hypothesis;
- best overall explanation of directionality;
- reasonable and substantial basis to investigate reckless reconstruction in 2025;
- knowing reconstruction unresolved;
- deliberate concealment/deception/fraud not proved.

## Claims that remain premature

Do not allege as established that:

- Monaghan or Counihan deliberately concealed known error;
- QPS, SIMT or the Medical Council knowingly reproduced a false account;
- actors conspired;
- records were fabricated;
- every foot symptom was neurological;
- immediate levodopa was clinically mandatory in 2017;
- Healy repudiated all delay;
- legal fraud is established.

The case is stronger without those propositions.
